Jobs

  1. Decide what members get for as long as they pay

    Forever Promise Framework · Robbie Kellman Baxter

    The process of defining the ongoing value commitment that turns a subscription into a real membership relationship.

    /aligned:use-framework forever-promise

  2. Get new users to build the habit

    3B Framework (Behavior, Barriers, Benefits) · Kristen Berman

    A behavioral-science diagnostic for product behavior problems that names the specific target behavior, audits five categories of barriers, and tests benefit salience to surface the single highest-leverage experiment.

    /aligned:use-framework 3b-behavioral-design

  3. Price on what members value

    Value Metric Framework · Patrick Campbell

    How their customer pays and what they pay for.

    /aligned:use-framework value-metric

  4. Check whether users would miss the product if it went away

    Product-Market Fit Test · Sean Ellis

    A diagnostic to determine if you should scale or iterate based on user love, not vanity metrics.

    /aligned:use-framework product-market-fit-test
