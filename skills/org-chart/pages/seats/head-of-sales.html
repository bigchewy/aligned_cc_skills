Jobs

  1. Find where revenue leaks between first touch and renewal

    Bowtie Funnel · Jacco van der Kooij

    A diagnostic walk through the end-to-end recurring-revenue architecture, six stages from Awareness through Expansion, to find where revenue is actually leaking.

    /aligned:use-framework bowtie-funnel

  2. Teach the team to sell with an insight instead of a demo

    Teach-Tailor-Take Control (TTTC) · Matt Dixon

    The three-skill Challenger sales motion, delivering a reframing commercial insight (Teach), adapting it to this specific buyer (Tailor), and assertively guiding commercial dynamics around pricing, timing, and next steps (Take Control).

    /aligned:use-framework teach-tailor-take-control

  3. Design the team that books first meetings

    6 Elements of the Sales Development Playbook · Trish Bertuzzi

    A full-function diagnostic and redesign of a B2B sales development organization across six design elements, Strategy, Specialization, Recruiting, Retention, Execution, and Leadership, producing a ranked fix list that stops leaders from hiring more SDRs into a broken design.

    /aligned:use-framework 6-elements-sdr

  4. If you sell into healthcare, pick your go-to-market motion

    5 GTM Motions for Digital Health · Julie Yoo

    A motion-commitment diagnostic that forces a digital health founder to pick one primary go-to-market motion, B2C2B, B2SMB, Risk-based contracting, Two-sided networks, or Distribution through aggregators, for the next 18 months, and confirms that product, evidence, buyer, and runway all match the chosen motion.

    /aligned:use-framework 5-gtm-motions-digital-health
