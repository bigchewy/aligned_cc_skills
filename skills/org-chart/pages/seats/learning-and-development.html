Builds training for managers and new hires.

Jobs

  1. Build training for a skill people use on the job

    Ten Steps Blueprint · Paul A. Kirschner

    Designing a program for a complex skill people use at work, built from whole tasks in classes from simple to complex, with support that fades, information given at the right time, and practice for the routines that must become automatic.

    /aligned:use-framework ten-steps-blueprint

  2. Check whether training is the fix at all

    Behavior Gap Diagnosis · Julie Dirksen

    Finding out why people aren't doing a target behavior, whether training can fix it, and which kind of fix matches each gap.

    /aligned:use-framework behavior-gap-diagnosis

  3. Decide what part AI plays in a lesson

    Assigning AI Roles · Ethan Mollick

    Choosing the one role an AI should play in a learning or coaching moment, naming that role's risk, and writing and testing a prompt whose guardrails keep the learner doing the thinking.

    /aligned:use-framework assigning-ai-roles

  4. Get a new practice to spread past the first few teams

    Complex Contagion Rollout · Damon Centola

    Planning how a costly, risky, or norm-breaking practice spreads through an organization, by choosing which tight groups adopt first, which groups go next based on how widely they are tied to groups that already adopted, and how many committed people each group needs before its norm flips.

    /aligned:use-framework complex-contagion-rollout

Ask an advisor

  • Paul A. Kirschner

    Emeritus Professor of Educational Psychology at the Open University of the Netherlands, co-author of Ten Steps to Complex Learning, How Learning Happens, and Evidence-Informed Learning Design; 4C/ID program design, cognitive load, and learning myths.

    /aligned:use-advisor paul-kirschner
  • Julie Dirksen

    Founder of Usable Learning, author of Design for How People Learn and Talk to the Elephant; learning design for behavior change, COM-B diagnosis, and when training is not the answer.

    /aligned:use-advisor julie-dirksen
  • Ethan Mollick

    Wharton professor, author of Co-Intelligence and the One Useful Thing newsletter; designing the AI's role in learning and coaching, with guardrailed prompts that keep the learner doing the thinking.

    /aligned:use-advisor ethan-mollick
  • Damon Centola

    Elihu Katz Professor at the University of Pennsylvania, director of the Network Dynamics Group, author of How Behavior Spreads and Change; how costly behavior spreads through clusters and wide bridges, and the order to seed it.

    /aligned:use-advisor damon-centola
