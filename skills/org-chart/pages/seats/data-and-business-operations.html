  1. Build a chart that makes one point

    Choose an Effective Visual · Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic

    Picking one chart type for one point by starting from the audience, naming the comparison the chart has to show, and then choosing bar, line, slopegraph or dot plot, or pie, with the reason and what was ruled out.

    /aligned:use-framework choose-an-effective-visual
    Other ways to do this
    • The Big Idea and Takeaway Title · Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic

      Deciding whether a page is for exploring or for explaining, naming who it is for and what they should know or do, telling the story in three minutes and then in one sentence, and rewriting every chart title as the takeaway, the "so what?"

      /aligned:use-framework big-idea-and-takeaway-title
    • Declutter and Focus · Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic

      Taking one existing chart, pushing everything to gray, cutting the clutter, then adding one accent color and a title that states the takeaway, and checking the result with the "where are your eyes drawn?" test, so the result is a list of changes to the chart, in order.

      /aligned:use-framework declutter-and-focus
