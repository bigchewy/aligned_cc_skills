Runs the operating rhythm of weekly updates and monthly reviews, and fixes processes that break.

Jobs

  1. Replace status meetings with a written weekly update

    The Weekly Check-In · Matt Mochary

    Replace status meetings with an async written update covering emotional state, single top priority, commitment review, and issues with proposed solutions.

    /aligned:use-framework mochary-weekly-check-in

  2. Run a monthly review that ends in decisions

    Operating Review Design · Claire Hughes Johnson

    Transform a status-presentation meeting into a decision-focused operating review through pre-reads, discussion-only meeting norms, decision logs, and strategy-connected metrics.

    /aligned:use-framework chj-operating-review

  3. Fix a process that keeps breaking

    The Algorithm · Elon Musk

    A strict five-step sequence for improving any process: question requirements, delete, simplify, accelerate, then automate.

    /aligned:use-framework the-algorithm
    Other ways to do this
    • 6-Step Process · Coach Eric

      A structured approach to solving operational problems that starts with a mindset check before goal-setting, diagnosis, and planning.

      /aligned:use-framework 6-step-process

  4. Turn a service your team delivers by hand into a package clients can buy

    Proven Process · Gino Wickman

    Turning a method someone delivers by hand into a repeatable engagement, with who it is for, the components it strengthens, a checkup that scores them, the named steps a client goes through, what gets written down so someone else can deliver it, and a one-page picture of the whole thing.

    /aligned:use-framework proven-process
