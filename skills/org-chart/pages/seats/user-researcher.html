User researcher

Product and designB2B: 1 personB2C: 1 personCovered

Runs customer interviews and usability tests and tells the team what they learned.

Jobs

  1. Run customer interviews that test what you think the problem is

    Customer Problem Presentation · Steve Blank

    Building a short presentation of the problems they think a customer has, so a customer meeting draws answers out of the customer instead of pitching to them.

    /aligned:use-framework customer-problem-presentation

  2. Find out what job customers hire the product for

    Jobs to Be Done · Clayton Christensen

    Uncovering the functional, emotional, and social progress a customer is hiring a product to make.

    /aligned:use-framework jobs-to-be-done

  3. Study how users decide, apart from what they say

    3B Framework (Behavior, Barriers, Benefits) · Kristen Berman

    A behavioral-science diagnostic for product behavior problems that names the specific target behavior, audits five categories of barriers, and tests benefit salience to surface the single highest-leverage experiment.

    /aligned:use-framework 3b-behavioral-design
