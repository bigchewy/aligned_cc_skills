  2. Write the messaging every page and pitch pulls from

    Messaging Distillation · April Dunford

    Distills positioning and narrative into a language/messaging.md file with slices for category name, tagline, elevator-pitch variants, value-prop phrasings, value-prop-to-persona map, key phrases, and (optional) pricing language.

    /aligned:use-framework messaging-distillation

  3. Arm the team against each competitor

    Competitive Battle Card · April Dunford

    Produces market/competitive.md with competitive-landscape, differentiators, and common-objections slices; declares depends_on against strategy/positioning#competitive-alternatives so drift is detectable.

    /aligned:use-framework competitive-battle-card

  4. Write down who buys and how they decide

    Buyer Persona · Matt Dixon

    Produces one personas/{role}.md file per run, capturing role overview, evaluation criteria, skepticism triggers, language resonance, and common objections, anchored in the Challenger commercial-insight frame.

    /aligned:use-framework buyer-persona

  5. Back every claim with a source and a date

    Proof Points Audit · Michael Bolton

    Produces proof/proof-points.md with structured, sourced, dated claims; conditionally splits into proof/clinical-evidence.md and proof/compliance.md when the org has clinical evidence or compliance certifications.

    /aligned:use-framework proof-points-audit

  6. Decide what customers pay for

    Value Metric Framework · Patrick Campbell

    How their customer pays and what they pay for.
