Product marketing

Writes the messaging, pricing pages, battle cards, and launch plans.

Jobs

  1. Write the messaging every page and pitch pulls from

    Messaging Distillation · April Dunford

    Distills positioning and narrative into a language/messaging.md file with slices for category name, tagline, elevator-pitch variants, value-prop phrasings, value-prop-to-persona map, key phrases, and (optional) pricing language.

    /aligned:use-framework messaging-distillation
    Other ways to do this
    • Origin Story Interview · April Dunford

      Extracting a founder's origin story and use cases for positioning.

      /aligned:use-framework origin-story-interview

  2. Arm the team against each competitor

    Competitive Battle Card · April Dunford

    Writing a battle card for each competitive alternative: the landscape, real advantages and disadvantages, myths to correct, and each common objection with the concern behind it, a response, and a question to ask.

    /aligned:use-framework competitive-battle-card

  3. Write down who buys and how they decide

    Buyer Persona · Matt Dixon

    Produces one personas/{role}.md file per run, capturing role overview, evaluation criteria, skepticism triggers, language resonance, and common objections, anchored in the Challenger commercial-insight frame.

    /aligned:use-framework buyer-persona

  4. Back every claim with a source and a date

    Proof Points Audit · Michael Bolton

    Pulling every claim out of customer-facing copy, tracing each to a source and date, rating how well it is supported, and reporting the claims that have no proof.

    /aligned:use-framework proof-points-audit

  5. Decide what customers pay for

    Value Metric Framework · Patrick Campbell

    Choosing the value metric a price scales with by brainstorming candidates, scoring them on alignment, simplicity, and growth, and planning how to validate the winner with customers.

    /aligned:use-framework value-metric
