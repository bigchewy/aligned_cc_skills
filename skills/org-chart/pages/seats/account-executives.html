  1. Tell the pitch as a story a buyer can act on

    Sales Pitch Structure · April Dunford

    Transforming positioning work into a story that helps buyers make confident decisions.

    /aligned:use-framework sales-pitch-structure

  2. Teach the buyer something new about their own business

    Teach-Tailor-Take Control (TTTC) · Matt Dixon

    The three-skill Challenger sales motion, delivering a reframing commercial insight (Teach), adapting it to this specific buyer (Tailor), and assertively guiding commercial dynamics around pricing, timing, and next steps (Take Control).

    /aligned:use-framework teach-tailor-take-control

  3. Find who on the buying side will push the deal through

    Mobilizer Taxonomy · Matt Dixon

    Assigning each person in a B2B buying committee to one of seven Dixon archetypes, separating the three Mobilizers (Go-Getter, Teacher, Skeptic) who drive consensus from the four Talkers (Guide, Friend, Climber, Blocker) who feel accessible but don't move deals.

    /aligned:use-framework mobilizer-taxonomy

  4. Save a deal that stalls because the buyer can't decide

    JOLT Effect · Matt Dixon

    A four-move tactical playbook for rescuing late-stage B2B enterprise deals stalled by customer indecision, Judge the indecision type, Offer a specific recommendation, Limit exploration, Take risk off the table.

    /aligned:use-framework jolt-effect
