FEW HICCUPPS Review

It · ProductMichael Bolton

Running a design doc, a feature, or an existing screen through the FEW HICCUPPS consistency oracles to find problems before they're built, and checking what the doc claims about the codebase against the code.

/aligned:use-framework few-hiccupps-review

Must have

  • a design doc, a feature description, or an existing screen to reviewfrom outside

Helps to have

  • the codebase the design doc describes, open so files can be read during the reviewfrom outside
  • the existing tests (checks) for the area the design touchesfrom outside
  • past bug reports or incidents in the same areafrom outside

Put the result into your Top Goal or your next check-in.
