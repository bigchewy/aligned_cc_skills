The Big Idea and Takeaway Title

It · DataCole Nussbaumer Knaflic

Deciding whether a page is for exploring or for explaining, naming who it is for and what they should know or do, telling the story in three minutes and then in one sentence, and rewriting every chart title as the takeaway, the "so what?"

/aligned:use-framework big-idea-and-takeaway-title

Must have

  • the page, report, slide or chart to work on, as an image, a file, a link, or a description of each chart and what it showsfrom outside

Helps to have

  • who will look at it, and what they decide or do after lookingfrom outside
  • the data behind each chart, or the numbers that matter mostfrom outside
  • the current title of each chartfrom outside
  • how it will be seen (a live dashboard, a sent report, a slide in a meeting, a phone screen)from outside
