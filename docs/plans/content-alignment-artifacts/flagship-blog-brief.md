# Flagship Blog Post Brief

## Title
**How to Run a Positioning Exercise with April Dunford at 2 AM**

## Primary Audience
Semi-technical leaders who have never used the aligned plugin but are curious about AI-augmented strategic thinking. They've heard "use AI for strategy" a hundred times but have never seen what that actually looks like with real methodology.

**Secondary:** Developers evaluating the methodology depth behind the plugin.

## Content Tilt
Nobody else shows recognized expert methodologies running as AI-native executable workflows with real output. Every AI article says "use AI for strategy." This piece shows April Dunford's actual framework running, with quality gates enforcing the process, producing real output. The tilt is the demonstration, not the claim.

**Filter:** If the piece doesn't show the system working with real methodology, the section doesn't belong.

## Hook
The metaphor IS the hook for content marketing (different from README's mechanism-first approach). "April Dunford at 2 AM" is what gets clicks. The mechanism is the payoff inside the piece.

## Voice
Match ewp-site blog voice: direct, CEO-coaching, systems-thinking. Not developer docs. Write like a CEO coach explaining what happened, not a product marketer selling features.

## Structure

### Opening (150-200 words)
Hook: It's late. You have a positioning problem. You've been going back and forth on how to describe your product for weeks. Generic AI gives you buzzword soup. You need someone who's helped 200+ tech companies position their products — but it's 2 AM.

Shift in the world: What if expert methodology wasn't locked behind $500/hour consultants and calendar availability?

### Section 1: The Setup (200-300 words)
What the aligned plugin is — brief, mechanism-first. 62 advisors, 135 frameworks, quality gates. Not generic AI roleplay. One paragraph max on what it is, then straight into the demonstration.

### Section 2: The Exercise (500-700 words)
Walk through an actual positioning exercise. Reference `docs/plugin-positioning.md` as the real output — this was a positioning exercise for the aligned plugin itself, run through the system.

Show:
- The structured 4-phase process (Goal → Problems → Root Causes → Solutions)
- How the system enforces the process order and won't let you skip steps
- April Dunford's 5 Components appearing in the critique panel
- Multiple advisors weighing in: Seth Godin on remarkability, Rob Walling on validation, Joe Pulizzi on content strategy
- The quality of the pushback — specific, methodology-driven, not generic

Key moment to highlight: The advisory panel insights from the actual exercise (Seth Godin: "The remarkable thing isn't the feature count — it's the opinion." Clayton Christensen: "This is disruptive in the precise sense." Rob Walling: "Validate with three organizations measuring before-and-after.")

### Section 3: What You'd Get from Generic AI (200-300 words)
Contrast. Ask ChatGPT to "help with positioning" and you get a template. Ask it to "be April Dunford" and you get a costume. No methodology. No quality gates. No pushback. No institutional knowledge.

The difference: methodology-driven AI vs. pattern-matching AI.

### Section 4: Why This Matters for Growing Companies (200-300 words)
Connect to the thesis: decision quality shouldn't depend on who's in the room. The new hire gets the same frameworks as the founder. The 2 AM session gets the same rigor as the Tuesday strategy meeting.

This is the ewp-site CEO-coaching voice section. Systems thinking about how organizations scale decision-making.

### Closing + CTA (100-150 words)
The plugin is open-source. Try it.

CTA: "One email when a new advisor or framework ships — no spam, no fluff." [newsletter signup link]

Link to the GitHub repo.
