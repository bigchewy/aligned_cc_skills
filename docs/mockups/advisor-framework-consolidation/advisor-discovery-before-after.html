Advisor Discovery: Before vs. After

Brainstorming session: advisor-framework-consolidation

Side-by-side swim-lane comparison of how use-advisor discovers advisor prompts. The BEFORE flow has 3 tiers of lookup (user home, directory.md, .repos manifest with per-repo globs). The AFTER flow is a single flat glob.

BEFORE: 3-Tier Discovery

    subgraph Tier2["Tier 2 — directory.md"]
