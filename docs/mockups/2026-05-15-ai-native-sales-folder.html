The AI-Native Sales Folder

A canonical sibling to brand/ — where generator agents read the reusable playbook a rep uses to MOVE A DEAL through 9–18-month digital-health enterprise cycles. Same shape, different fills across subdomains and buyer-side mixes.

Date 2026-05-15 Topic ai-native-sales-folder Mode Software Revision v0.2 (post-critique) Backbone: Challenger / JOLT / Challenger Customer (Dixon)

01Why now

brand/ tells generators what is true about us. But a deal does not move on truth alone. A deal moves when a rep teaches a buyer something they didn't know about their own business, neutralizes the buying-committee dynamics that cause indecision, and removes the four reasons a buyer would say "let's wait." That is the playbook layer — and right now it lives in reps' heads, scattered slack messages, and a few half-finished battle cards.

Generators are reaching past brand/

Sales reps want a deck that teaches a reframe tailored to this CMIO, anticipates this committee's blocker, and includes a mutual close plan procurement can sign. None of that lives in brand/.

Dixon's three books name the slots

The Challenger Sale, The Challenger Customer, and The JOLT Effect collectively define the surface: commercial insight, the 7 stakeholder profiles, the 6.8-buyer committee, the 4 indecision drivers, the Mobilizer / Talker / Blocker taxonomy, the Teach / Tailor / Take-Control sequence.

clients/ already needed a playbook

The brand-folder design carved out clients/{prospect}.md for transient deal data. Without a playbook to compose against, every prospect file becomes a copy-paste of the previous one. sales/ is that playbook.

This is not CRM. CRM tracks the deal. sales/ carries the way we sell — the reusable artifacts every deal composes against. Per-deal data stays in clients/. Durable thesis stays in brand/. The reusable playbook lives here.

02Goal

Define the canonical sales/ folder that an AI-native digital health company installs once, and from which every sales-asset generator — discovery preps, exec briefs, mutual close plans, deal-stage decks, objection handlers, indecision-driver responses, references-playbook lookups — reads predictably.

  • Work uniformly across digital-health subdomains and buyer-side mixes (provider / payer / employer / direct-to-consumer Rx).
  • Compose against brand/ by reference (no duplication of personas, proof, competitive content).
  • Compose against clients/{prospect} as a per-deal overlay (single-file or folder shape).
  • Encode provenance (status + confidence) at the same bar as brand/, plus one sales-only field that surfaces playbook drift.
  • Ship a manifest that tells the next thread what to deepen next.

03Hard constraints

Rules the architecture must honor in every implementation. Each constraint is enforceable — a generator, a lint, or a schema check fails if it's violated.

Sibling to brand/, not nested under it. sales/ is a top-level peer at the same level as brand/ and clients/. Generators that previously read only brand/ now read all three — one loader.
One shape, every digital-health company. Identical directory layout across telehealth, RPM, behavioral health, digital therapeutics, payer / provider / employer mixes. Subdomains shift the fills, never the slots.
No content duplication with brand/. Personas, proof, competitive content live in brand/. sales/ references them by slug via depends_on:. Concern-keyed objections in sales/ reference competitor-keyed comparisons in brand/ — they don't try to be each other.
Per-deal state stays in clients/. sales/ holds reusable templates and the playbook of plays. Both clients/{prospect}.md (single-file, dispatch-tracker precedent) and clients/{prospect}/ (folder, planted precedent) shapes are valid.
AI-first design. Primary consumer is generator agents. Optimize for machine-parseable structure, terse summaries, dense slice tagging.
Frontmatter mandatory on every .md. Same schema as brand/, plus 3 optional sales-only fields.
Provenance + confidence — same scheme as brand/. status: draft | validated | retired. confidence: low | medium | high. Plus last_used_in_deal: for playbook-drift detection.
Slice-loadable by H2. {file}#{slug}. No nested H2-as-decoration.
One loader, not three. v1 generalizes skills/_shared/load-brand-slices.md → skills/_shared/load-slices.md with a tree: parameter (brand | sales | clients).
Composition order is fixed. sales/ (playbook) → brand/ (thesis) → clients/{prospect} (deal). Reverse lets a per-deal hack override company truth.
No CRM emulation. sales/ does not mirror opportunity stages, dollar amounts, forecast dates, or contact records. clients/{prospect} carries a crm_link: reference.
No legal text or contract clauses. Contracts live in their own system. sales/ carries the rep's playbook for navigating legal/security review, not the contracts themselves.
Clean-state cutover. No legacy aliases. v1 ships the new layout; existing sales-asset generators (if any) are updated in the same change.

04Core commitments

The worldview the design rests on. Softer than hard constraints — these are the principles that explain why the architecture is shaped the way it is.

The three-folder triangle

Three folders, three jobs, three change cadences:

  • brand/ — the WHY. Durable thesis. Changes ~2× per year. Customer-agnostic.
  • sales/ — the HOW. Reusable playbook for moving a deal. Changes when reps learn what's working. Customer-aware (vertical / persona / stage), but not deal-specific.
  • clients/{prospect} — the THIS DEAL. Per-prospect instance. Changes constantly inside an active cycle. Deal-specific.

Three change cadences = three folders = three review processes. Generators always compose all three.

Concern-keyed, not competitor-keyed

Objections are organized by what the buyer is worried about (pricing, timing, risk, incumbency, authority, internal-politics) — not by competitor. Competitor talk-tracks live in brand/market/competitive.md and are referenced when relevant.

Profile is orthogonal to role

A CMIO can be Mobilizer or Blocker. The 7-profile taxonomy lives in sales/buyers/stakeholder-profiles.md. The typical role↔profile distribution lives in by-persona/{role}.md with confidence labels — not pretending the role determines the profile.

Cost-of-inaction has two halves

The evidence (industry numbers + customer outcomes) lives in brand/proof/cost-of-inaction-evidence.md. The playbook (calculator script + reframe sequence + the silence after the number lands) lives in sales/insight/cost-of-inaction.md.

Commercial insight ≠ narrative

Narrative is the macro shift, customer-agnostic, durable. Commercial insight is buyer-surprise-tuned, gets refined as new data lands. They cross-reference via depends_on: but live independently.

Deal stages reusable, status per-deal

sales/stages/{stage}.md defines what TEACH / TAILOR / TAKE-CONTROL are. clients/{prospect}#current_stage declares where one deal sits.

Mobilizer is the unit of progress

A deal with an identified Mobilizer is a different stage than a deal whose champion is a Talker. buying-committee.md defines dynamics; clients/{prospect}#mobilizer_id declares the person.

11Resolved questions

The seven design questions you asked, with the choice taken and the alternative considered. Each is grounded in the architecture above.

v0.2 critique-applied corrections affecting this section: The 5-step Teach pitch in Q1 is now 6 steps with The Warmer added (Dixon caught this). The 4 indecision drivers in Q-related discussion are now 3 drivers (Outcome Uncertainty, Valuation Problems, Lack of Information — not "Information Burden" which is the opposite polarity term). Anticipated Regret is relocated as JOLT's T response (Take fear off the table), not a fourth driver. The cost-of-inaction "calculator" framing in Q4 is renamed to the Rational Drowning mechanic (Dixon's teaching mechanic — make the buyer feel the number, not run a spreadsheet).

Q1 · Where does the Challenger commercial insight live?

Choice: sales/insight/commercial-insight.md, with depends_on: [brand/strategy/narrative#shift-in-the-world, brand/proof/cost-of-inaction-evidence]. Overlap with narrative is real but they are not the same artifact: narrative is customer-agnostic and durable (the macro shift the company sees), commercial insight is buyer-surprise-tuned and gets refined as new data lands (the in-the-room reframe). Different change cadences, different generators consume each.

Alternative considered: fuse them into a single narrative-and-insight.md. Rejected — couples cadences, and a marketing generator (which wants the durable arc) and a sales-deck generator (which wants the in-room reframe) would have to filter the same file. Two files, one depends_on: is cleaner.

Q2 · How do per-persona discovery questions compose with brand/personas/{role}.md?

Choice: Reference by slug, never duplicate. sales/discovery/by-persona/{role}.md declares depends_on: [brand/personas/{role}#kpis, brand/personas/{role}#language, brand/personas/{role}#objections]. The loader merges role definition (from brand) with discovery questions (from sales) at runtime.

Alternative considered: duplicate the role's KPIs into the sales discovery file for self-containment. Rejected — violates the no-duplication constraint and creates drift the moment brand updates a KPI.

Q3 · Mobilizer / Talker / Blocker taxonomy per role — where, and how kept in sync?

Choice: Two-file split. sales/buyers/stakeholder-profiles.md carries the taxonomy itself (one slice per profile: mobilizer-go-getter, mobilizer-teacher, mobilizer-skeptic, talker-friend, talker-climber, talker-guide, blocker). sales/buyers/by-persona/{role}.md carries the typical distribution for this role, with confidence labels — because a CMIO is Mobilizer-Teacher some of the time, not always.

Sync mechanism: an eval scenario asserts every brand/personas/{role}.md has a corresponding sales/buyers/by-persona/{role}.md, OR the role declares sales_relevant: false in its frontmatter.

Alternative considered: embed the profile distribution inside brand/personas/{role}.md. Rejected — collapses the orthogonality the Challenger Customer book is built on (profile is per-individual, role is per-job-title).

Q4 · Cost-of-inaction by vertical — sales/ or proof/?

Choice: Split. brand/proof/cost-of-inaction-evidence.md carries the verifiable industry numbers and customer outcomes (sourced, dated). sales/insight/cost-of-inaction.md carries the playbook for using the numbers in a deal — discovery sequence, calculator script, the deliberate silence after the number lands.

The split mirrors how brand/proof/case-studies.md (evidence) relates to sales/references/playbook.md (deployment).

Alternative considered: keep it all in brand/proof/. Rejected — muddles two different artifacts (verifiable claim vs. rep playbook) that update at different cadences and serve different consumers.

Q5 · Objection library keyed by concern, not competitor

Choice: Yes, fully distinct from brand/market/competitive.md. Six concern-keyed files in sales/objections/: pricing, timing, risk, incumbency, authority, internal-politics. Each H2 is one specific objection with responses per stakeholder profile. When an objection involves a competitor, the response references brand/market/competitive.md#vs-{competitor}. The two never collapse.

Alternative considered: one objection file per competitor. Rejected — doesn't scale (every new competitor needs a new file), and most objections aren't competitor-specific (timing, authority, internal politics happen regardless of who else is in the deal).

Q6 · References playbook — deal-stage and objection-type indexing

Choice: sales/references/playbook.md is a multi-axis matrix indexed by case-study × objection × stage × persona × vertical. Each row is one usable reference recommendation. sales/references/protocols/{customer}.md holds per-customer protocol detail (legal status / named-reference clearance, intro path through CSM, preferred meeting style, last-talked-to date for freshness). Composes against brand/proof/case-studies.md by slug.

Alternative considered: tag each case study in brand/proof/case-studies.md with objection/stage/persona metadata. Rejected — pollutes brand artifacts with sales-cycle metadata that brand consumers (decks, blog posts) don't need; metadata cadence (which references are still warm) is sales-side, not brand-side.

Q7 · Deal-stage assets — Challenger TEACH/TAILOR/TAKE-CONTROL or Bowtie?

Choice: Five pre-close stages that absorb both: discovery | teach | tailor | take-control | close. Each in sales/stages/{stage}.md. Mapping:

  • Discovery = Bowtie's Awareness/Education — the rep finding the deal and qualifying.
  • Teach = Challenger's Teach + JOLT's Judge the indecision level.
  • Tailor = Challenger's Tailor + JOLT's Offer your recommendation.
  • Take-Control = Challenger's Take Control + JOLT's Limit the exploration.
  • Close = Bowtie's Selection→Commit + JOLT's Take fear of failure off the table.

Onboarding / Adopt / Expand / Renewal are post-close and live in clients/{prospect} lifecycle, not sales/.

Alternative considered: use Bowtie's eight stages directly. Rejected — pre-close granularity matters more than post-close granularity for the playbook layer (post-close is per-deal lifecycle, not reusable plays); the five chosen stages give every stage a Challenger-or-JOLT verb attached.

15Orphan / residual catalog

What was considered during design and excluded, with the one-line reason for each. Prevents future revisits of settled-and-rejected ideas.

  • Sales folder nested under brand/sales/ — couples lifecycles that should change independently.
  • One unified objection-library file — not slice-loadable at the concern granularity generators need.
  • Per-competitor objection files — doesn't scale; most objections aren't competitor-specific.
  • Profile distribution embedded in brand/personas/{role}.md — collapses orthogonality of profile vs. role.
  • Cost-of-inaction in sales/ only — separates evidence from playbook; data updates independently from script.
  • Cost-of-inaction in brand/proof/ only — muddles two artifacts (verifiable claim vs. rep playbook) with different consumers.
  • Bowtie's eight stages directly — pre-close granularity matters for the playbook layer; post-close is per-deal lifecycle.
  • Onboarding / Adopt / Expand / Renewal as sales/stages/ entries — those are per-deal lifecycle, live in clients/{prospect}.
  • Mirroring CRM opportunity stage / forecast / dollar amount in clients/{prospect} — produces drift; CRM is system of record. Reference via crm_link:.
  • Contract clauses or legal templates in sales/ — live in their own system; sales/ carries the playbook for navigating legal review, not the contracts.
  • Per-rep playbook variants — playbook drift is solved by last_used_in_deal: + retiring slices, not by fragmenting the canonical playbook.
  • Talk-track audio/video clips in v1 — text-only for v1 (open question for later).
  • BANT / GPCT / MEDDIC alternatives to MEDDPICC in v1 — single qualification rubric per install; alternatives are open question.
  • Three separate loaders (brand-loader / sales-loader / clients-loader) — one parameterized loader matches the brand mockup's "one loader, not six" rule.
  • Sales contracts.yaml redefining brand generators — sales contracts only declare sales-asset generators; brand generators stay in brand contracts.

17Migration & adoption

How to ship this design. There are no existing sales-asset generators in the canonical plugin (the project scan confirmed). The risk surface is the brand-loader rename — existing brand generators (generate-deck, generate-blog-post, generate-one-pager) reference the loader path. They migrate in the same coordinated PR.

Single-PR rollout

  1. Rename the loader. skills/_shared/load-brand-slices.md → skills/_shared/load-slices.md with a tree: parameter (brand | sales | clients). Parsing primitives unchanged.
  2. Update brand-side generators. generate-deck, generate-blog-post, generate-one-pager get the new loader path + the explicit tree: brand parameter. No content changes.
  3. Ship the sales contracts.yaml and CLAUDE.md template. Empty sales/ folder is valid (loader gracefully degrades).
  4. Register the 6 net-new frameworks + orchestrator in frameworks/registry.yaml.
  5. Add the 10 new evals as eval-scenario entries.
  6. Lint enabled from day one. Frontmatter required-field lint, cross-folder depends_on: resolution lint, sales-relevance-coverage lint all fail the build on violation.
  7. Reference instance backfill (optional, follow-on PR). Populate dispatch-tracker/sales/ via reverse-engineered-sales as a structural validation.

No deprecation window. The brand loader move is in-place; no aliasing shim left behind.
