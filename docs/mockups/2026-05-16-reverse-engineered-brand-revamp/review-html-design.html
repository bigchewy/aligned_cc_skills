Raw material

We received 11 sources spanning a Q4 2024 investor pitch deck, three sales call transcripts (Q1 2025), the public marketing site, two competitor-alternatives pages, a 2023 partnership announcement, and an unstructured Slack export of internal positioning debate. Decks gave us strong signal on category framing and the product feature surface; transcripts revealed the actual buyer committee but only for 3 of the 5 inferred persona archetypes; the website was scarce on positioning specifics — most copy is feature-led, not buyer-problem-led.

Primary research

We identified 3 likely competitors from cross-source mention frequency (DispatchTrack, Roundtrip, Kaizen Health) and pulled public dossiers on each — positioning one-liners, ICP, channels, voice, narrative. The dossiers gave us strong category-shape signal: every named competitor sells to integrated health systems or ACOs, every one anchors on transport reliability or outcomes. What's less clear from the dossiers is Marley's specific differentiator — public competitive context shows the category, not Marley's wedge inside it.

Market category

Marley is a care-coordination platform for last-mile health logistics — combining patient transport scheduling, medication adherence prompts, and post-discharge follow-up in a single workflow surface.

Customer alternatives

Spreadsheets + manual dispatch (most common), incumbent NEMT brokers (LogistiCare, MTM), and in some cases nothing (post-discharge calls are skipped).

Unique value

EHR-integrated workflow: transport coordinators see the same patient record as the discharge nurse, reducing the inter-departmental handoff that's the #1 cause of missed rides.

Differentiated features

Bi-directional Epic + Cerner integration, real-time transport tracking with ETA push to the care team, automated 72-hour follow-up call queue keyed off discharge.
