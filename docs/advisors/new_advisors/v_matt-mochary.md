## Frameworks (Curation Candidates)

### 1. The Weekly Check-In (TOP CANDIDATE)

Async written update replacing status meetings. Template has four sections:
1. **FAJG** — Fears, Anger/Frustration, Joy/Wins, Gratitude (emotional state check)
2. **Top Goal** — One thing that matters most this week, and progress vs. last week
3. **Commitments Review** — What was committed last week (completed / not completed / why), and commitments for this week
4. **Issues for Discussion** — Items needing team input, each with a proposed solution

Shared 24 hours before the weekly meeting. Meeting time is for decisions only, not reading.

**Selection score:** Highly distinctive, directly applicable to the user's operating cadence use case, repeatable weekly.

### 2. Top Goal

One single most important outcome per week. Written, visible to the team. The CEO's Top Goal *is* the company's North Star for the week — if their Top Goal and a report's Top Goal conflict, something is misaligned. Tracked daily.

**Selection score:** Simple enough to run standalone; also powers the Weekly Check-In.

### 3. Issues + Proposed Solutions (1-3-1)

Rule: never bring a problem to a leader without a proposed solution. Structure: 1 issue, 3 options, 1 recommendation. Prevents learned helplessness and upward delegation.

**Selection score:** Fits into the Weekly Check-In's Issues section; can also be run as a standalone habit-building framework.

### 4. Energy Audit

Map all work activities on two axes: energy-giving vs. energy-draining, and high-importance vs. low-importance. Identify what's draining that can be delegated. Goal: CEOs should spend 75%+ of time in Zone of Genius (high energy + high skill). 

**Selection score:** Distinctive, important for executive coaching, but secondary to operating cadence use case.

### 5. Direct Communication Rule

All feedback goes directly to the person. Venting to a third party is explicitly prohibited — it spreads negativity and violates the direct communication norm. If you have feedback, write it down and send it directly. No triangulation.

**Selection score:** Cross-cuts all Mochary frameworks; best embedded in the advisor voice rather than a standalone framework.

### 6. OKR Construction and Quarterly Rhythm

Quarterly Objectives + Key Results, with weekly check-in progress tied to KRs. Mochary's version is closely tied to his Weekly Check-In: the Top Goal each week traces back to a KR. Quarterly planning sessions set OKRs; weekly check-ins maintain accountability.

**Selection score:** Reusable for the operating cadence use case; fits alongside Doerr's framework as a complementary "rhythm" layer.
