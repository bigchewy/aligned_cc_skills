Notes for adapting it:
- Questions 3, 4, 5, 7 and 10 each compare against "the Top Ranked Pages for Target Queries." The checklist is
  comparative, not absolute. The first move is to name the target query and look at who ranks. ✅ (wording above)
- The order is technical first. If a page can't be crawled or indexed, nothing after question 2 matters. That
  conclusion is the obvious reading of the order, not a line she wrote. 🟡
- The "URL Parameters tool" in question 1 was retired by Google in 2022 🟡 [GSC-URLPARAM]. The checklist's tool
  list is older than the page's copyright footer. Don't send users to it.
- Free tools that cover the questions for one person: Search Console (crawl and index status, sitemaps, links,
  manual actions, search queries and click-through) ✅ [GSC-ABOUT] (capabilities, not report names). Report
  names "URL Inspection," "Page indexing," "Sitemaps," "Manual actions," "Core Web Vitals," "Performance"
  are standard Search Console names 🟡 (third-party guides via search) / 🧠. PageSpeed Insights for Core Web
  Vitals 🧠. A plain Google search for the target query for questions 3, 4 and 10 ✅ ("Use Google Search
  Results").
- For a brand-new domain: question 8 (disavow) rarely applies, since a new owner has disavowed nothing.
  Question 9 (manual action) rarely applies to a clean new domain, but does apply to a bought or expired domain
  with history. Question 7 (links) is the one a new site almost always loses, and it can't be fixed in an hour.
  These are adaptation judgments for this plugin, not her words. 🟡

### The AI Search Optimization Framework & Checklist ([AS-AICHECK], updated 26 Sep 2026)

The brief cited a May 2026 version read only through a ppc.land summary. The live page was read this session
and is now the September 2026 version, with twelve steps. Title: "The AI Search Optimization Framework &
Checklist – With Worksheet [Updated Sep 2026]" ✅.

Framing, ✅:
- "If you want to successfully improve your AI search visibility in a consistent, durable and impactful way,"
  "you need to establish an aligned process and criteria taking into account your marketing and business goals."
- "The AI search optimization checklist runs on top of my 3 Layer Framework to Measure AI Search Presence,
  Readiness and Business Impact"
- "Steps 1 to 2 measure AI search presence" "Steps 3 to 10 diagnose and fix readiness, owned content,
  extractability, entities," "third-party corroboration, commercial data and localization." "Steps 11 to 12
  report AI business impact and validate whether the expected outcome is achieved"

The twelve steps, with her key question, actions and target outcome (all ✅ short excerpts):

1. **"Establish the prompts and journeys you actually want to influence in AI systems"** (Presence). Key
   question: "Are we measuring representative, commercially relevant decisions across the customer journey?"
   Actions: "Build the coverage matrix before writing prompts." "Start with 30-50 tagged core prompts and keep
   experimental prompts separate." Outcome: "Prompt library coverage across priority products, audiences,
   journeys and markets"
2. **"Measure your initial AI presence in popular AI systems before implementation"** (Presence). Key question:
   "Do we appear, get recommended, receive a link, win comparisons and get described accurately". Actions: "Run
   each core prompt three to five times within a fixed 24-72 hour window." "Document platform, market, location,
   language, account state and model or experience." "Record appearance, recommendation, link, comparative
   preference, accuracy, sources and competitors." Outcome: "Prompt coverage; recommendation rate; linked
   citation rate; comparative win rate; representation accuracy"
3. **"Diagnose your brand AI search presence gap before taking action"** (Readiness). Key question: "Which
   readiness / optimization characteristic or condition best explains the observed AI presence pattern across
   platforms?" Actions: "Map every meaningful AI search presence pattern to one primary readiness or source
   hypothesis:" "Record the evidence, affected segment, owner and expected KPI." "Prioritize with Severity x
   Importance / Effort." Guide: "The 10 Key Characteristics of AI Search Winning Brands"
4. **"Make priority pages retrievable and extractable for AI systems"** (Readiness). Key question: "Can search
   and AI systems reach, render and isolate the information needed for target prompts and topics?" Actions:
   "Audit priority URLs for crawlability, indexability, rendering, internal linking, etc. for key AI systems."
   "Feature critical facts, content and links in raw HTML." "Use descriptive headings, direct answers, named
   entities, explicit criteria and self-contained tables." "Validate after implementation."
5. **"Align your brand entity, naming and positioning signals"** (Readiness). Key question: "Are the brand,
   products, category, audience, people and markets described explicitly and consistently" "across the on-page
   and third-party sources that influence AI answers?" Actions: "Create a dated, approved fact sheet covering
   official names, product and category descriptions," "Compare it with visible copy, structured data,
   listings, review platforms, partner pages" "Correct stale or conflicting sources and re-assess accuracy."
6. **"Create decision support content and prioritize what still has a role after the AI answer"** (Readiness).
   Key question: "What unresolved need gives the user, publisher or AI system a reason to visit, cite or
   associate". Actions: "Audit relevant pages by target prompt and page role." "Score click resilience,
   citation potential, brand mention potential, business value," "Optimize or create comparisons, alternatives,
   pricing, specifications, documentation, support," "Connect evidence pages to the appropriate next action."
7. **"Grow your brand’s third-party AI source ecosystem"** (Readiness). Key question: "Which external sources
   repeatedly validate, compare, demonstrate or challenge the brand for priority prompt groups". Actions: "Map
   cited domains and source types separately by platform, vertical and market." "Correct inaccuracies and assign
   actions across PR, reviews, community, creators, partnerships and reputation."
8. **"Make commercial and transactional information machine-readable"** (Readiness). Key question: "Can users
   and AI systems understand your products’ offer, constraints, current state and next action without
   guessing?" Action: "Validate product names, categories, prices, currency, stock, variants, plans, features,
   limits, eligibility" "across visible pages, structured data, feeds, merchant programs, marketplaces, partner
   listings and app stores"
9. **"Localize your presence by market, not only by language"** (Readiness). Key question: "Does the prompt,
   content, source and competitor system reflect how people ask, trust, compare and act" "in each targeted
   country market?" Action: "Build separate modules for each priority targeted country market."
10. **"Classify and measure what earns citations and what attracts clicks separately"** (Readiness). Key
    question: "Are we building the evidence AI systems can cite and the destinations users still need to visit".
    Actions: "Classify cited URLs and AI traffic destinations by role and owner: brand entry, discovery/evaluation"
    "Filter authentication, redirect, payment and other operational noise before marketing analysis."
11. **"Report AI search optimization efforts and outcomes without overclaiming"** (Impact). Key question: "Can
    the report explain where the brand appears, why it appears that way and whether it creates value" "with the
    right confidence label on each number?" Action: "Report AI presence, readiness and business impact as
    connected but separate layers."
12. **"Run a recurring AI validation and optimization loop"** (Impact). Key question: "After each
    implementation, did the expected presence, source, representation or business signal move". Actions:
    "Rerun your stable core prompt groups under the documented protocol." "Keep, expand, revise or stop the
    action based on evidence."

### Proposed phases for `why-my-page-doesnt-rank`

1. The page and the target query; search it and name the top ranked pages.
2. Questions 1-2: crawlable and indexable (URL Inspection). Stop here if it fails.
3. Question 3: more relevant than the top ranked pages.
4. Question 4: more helpful, comprehensive, authoritative.
5. Question 5: user experience (PageSpeed Insights, HTTPS, mobile, interstitials).
6. Question 6: internal links and XML sitemap.
7. Questions 7-9: links, disavow, manual action. Say which rarely apply to a new domain.
8. Question 10: the search result itself (title, snippet, structured data; CTR in Performance).
9. One fix first, then a short list; recheck date.

### Proposed phases for `ai-search-optimization-checklist`

One phase per step, twelve in all, scaled for a small site: 10-20 prompts instead of 30-50, two or three AI
systems, a spreadsheet instead of a tool. Steps 8 and 9 rarely apply to a non-commerce, single-country site;
step 10 has little data on a new site. Refuse prompt injection, fabricated reviews and citation gaming.
