## Who

- Created EOS, the Entrepreneurial Operating System. Founded EOS Worldwide with Don Tinney; it
  "officially launched" in 2008 with eight implementers. ✅ (History)
- Became an entrepreneur at 21, took over the family business at 25 when it was in debt, ran it about
  seven years, and sold it. 🟡 (search summary of ginowickman.com/about; the page fetch did not show it).
  His father built a real estate sales training company. ✅ (EOS Life pod p.4)
- Built EOS from his own sessions: "I spent those 5 years working with 50 companies, 500 sessions, and
  I just listened to all their problems." ✅ (MS) Finished EOS in 2006, published *Traction* in 2007 🟡.
- Got the idea in an entrepreneurs' forum (YEO, now EO): "these guys are as screwed up as I am. And I saw
  opportunity." ✅ (EOS Life pod p.2)
- Stepped back from day-to-day work at EOS Worldwide. Mark O'Donnell (Visionary) and Kelly Knight
  (Integrator) run it now. ✅ (History) The Firefly Group bought a majority stake; Wickman and Tinney kept
  ownership interests 🟡 (Amherst Partners deal note, search summary).
- Now spends his time "speaking, writing, coaching, podcasting, and teaching." Mission: "To empower
  entrepreneurs and leaders to maximize their freedom, creativity, and impact." ✅ (GW About)
- Usage figures change by source and date: "almost 100,000 companies using those tools" ✅ (MS, undated);
  "used by over 250,000 businesses worldwide" ✅ (GW EOS, current). Use the current one with its source.

### How EOS Worldwide made it a business

- **Books as the front door.** The "Traction Library": books for the owner, the leadership team, the
  Visionary/Integrator pair, managers, and employees. ✅ (titles above)
- **Free tools.** V/TO, Accountability Chart, Scorecard, Rocks, Level 10 agenda, People Analyzer, GWC,
  Delegate and Elevate, Organizational Checkup and more, free to download. ✅ (Tools)
- **Free first meeting.** The 90-Minute Meeting costs nothing. ✅ (Ninety glossary)
- **Implementers as a franchise network.** The site calls it a franchise; "1,000+ coaches"; Boot Camp
  before launch; "The $400K+ System" aimed at $400,000 with 20 clients. ✅ (Implementer) Professional,
  Certified, and Expert levels exist 🟡 (not seen on that page).
- **Trademarks on every tool name.** V/TO®, Level 10 Meeting™, Focus Day®, Vision Building®, Six Key
  Components™, Accountability Chart™, GWC™. ✅ (marks on EOS pages) Anyone building their own system
  has to name their own tools.
- **Software through licenses, then their own.** Traction Tools was licensed from 2013 until EOS
  Worldwide ended the license in 2022 "as EOS builds its own software"; it rebranded as Bloom Growth.
  ✅ (Bloom PR) Ninety signed a multi-year license in March 2023 as "the only licensed software
  provider." ✅ (Ninety PR) EOS One "is built by EOS Worldwide." ✅ (EOS One) Whether Ninety's license is
  still current was not checked.

## Where EOS touches trust, team health, and self-awareness

- **Healthy** is one of the three outcomes (vision, traction, healthy). ✅
- **Checkup statement 11:** "Our leadership team is open and honest, and demonstrates a high level of
  trust." One statement of twenty. ✅
- **Vision Building Day 1** and **Annual Day 1** open with team health. ✅ (Ninety glossary) Ninety's
  blog describes Lencioni's Five Dysfunctions and the Personal Histories exercise as common team-health
  exercises. 🟡 (Ninety blog; not seen as required EOS content)
- **Core Values and the People Analyzer** are the culture tools. "Feels icky, to give you a scientific
  word" is his description of a culture without them. ✅ (EOS Life pod p.7)
- **The Segue** opens every weekly meeting with personal and business good news. ✅ It is a check-in on
  wins, not on state or presence.
- **The EOS Life and Shine** are his own turn inward: "It is possible to be driven and have inner peace."
  ✅ (GW 10 Disciplines) The 10 disciplines include Know Thyself and Be Still (sit in silence 30 minutes a
  day) 🟡 (podcast episode titles and summaries). He says of himself: "I'm not a psychologist." ✅
- **How prominent:** low. Trust is one statement in the checkup and a block of time on two session days.
  The inner work sits in separate books (*Shine*, *The EOS Life*) that are not part of the EOS Process.
  Third-party reviews call EOS rigid and light on emotion 🟡 (monsterops.io review; search summaries).
  This is the gap Eric's I and We are aimed at.

## Voice

- Spoken, warm, plain, a little salty: "holy cow," "bust my ass," "feels icky, to give you a scientific
  word," "you can't fire all of your family members."
- A teacher who simplifies and repeats: "it's this simple," "it's so common sense," "long story short."
- Numbers and percentages everywhere: 20/80, 100%, rate yourself 1 to 10, 50% to 52% next quarter.
- Owns his own mess: "I am screwed up, you are screwed up."
- Pushes discipline over insight: "we force you to implement this tool."
- Small steps: "one little move in the right direction," "one thing per quarter."
