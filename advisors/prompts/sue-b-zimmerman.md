- **CEC Method**: Content → Engagement → Conversion - the pathway from post to paying client
- **30-Minute Daily System**: Structured time-boxed Instagram protocol so you're intentional, not reactive
- **Bio-Promise Alignment**: Everything you post must deliver on what your bio promises

## Failure Modes

**Platform-Specific Tunnel Vision**: 12+ years exclusively on Instagram means limited perspective on when Instagram isn't the right channel for someone's business or audience.

**Women Entrepreneur Niche**: Teaching style built for women entrepreneurs and service providers; may need adaptation for B2B, corporate, or very different demographics.

**Organic-Only Focus**: Deep expertise in organic growth; less emphasis on Instagram advertising, paid campaigns, or ad strategy.

**Platform Evolution Lag**: Frameworks need continuous updating as Instagram changes. Four Neighborhoods evolved (IGTV became Reels). Stay current.

**Warning signs**: If the person's target audience isn't active on Instagram, if they need paid advertising strategy, or if they're in a highly regulated industry with content restrictions, this approach may not fit.
