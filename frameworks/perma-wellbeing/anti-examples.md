# PERMA Wellbeing Anti-Examples

### Treating low scores as problems to fix

**User:** "My positive emotion score is a 3. That's terrible."

**Wrong:** "That is concerning. Low positive emotion is associated with depression. We need to work on increasing your happiness through gratitude journaling and positive activities."

**Right:** "A 3 is important data, not a diagnosis. Positive emotion is one of five pillars. Some people naturally run lower here and flourish through engagement or meaning instead. Let's see the full picture before deciding where to focus. What about the other dimensions?"

> PERMA isn't about maximizing all five - it's about understanding your profile. Some people flourish with lower positive emotion but high meaning.

### Making it feel like a test

**User:** "What's a good score for relationships?"

**Wrong:** "Research suggests people with high wellbeing typically score 7 or above on relationships. Below 5 indicates a significant deficit that should be addressed."

**Right:** "There's no 'good' score. This is about your own assessment - how connected do YOU feel? The number helps us compare across your own dimensions, not against some standard. What feels true for you?"

> PERMA is a self-assessment tool, not a diagnostic with clinical cutoffs. Making it feel like a test creates performance anxiety.

### Ignoring the connections between pillars

**User:** "My engagement is low. How do I increase engagement?"

**Wrong:** "Let's focus on finding more flow activities. What activities have put you in flow in the past? Let's schedule more of those."

**Right:** "Low engagement is worth understanding. But before we isolate it, let's see how it connects to the other pillars. Often low engagement links to low meaning - when work feels pointless, flow disappears. Or low positive emotion - when you're depleted, nothing absorbs you. What else is low in your profile?"

> The five pillars interact. Low engagement might be caused by problems in other dimensions. Treating them in isolation misses the systemic picture.
