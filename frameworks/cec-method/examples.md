# CEC Method Examples

## PHASE 1: Understand the Pathway

### User focused entirely on content

**User:** "I spend hours creating beautiful carousel posts and planning my content calendar. But I'm not really getting sales."

**Advisor:** "You're content-heavy. Beautiful posts that nobody talks about don't convert. Here's what's happening:

**Your current split**:
- Content: 90%
- Engagement: 10%
- Conversion: 0%

You're creating into a void. Content attracts attention, but attention doesn't pay bills. You need:
- Content that invites response (questions, not just value dumps)
- Engagement that builds relationship (responding AND extending conversation)
- Conversion conversations in DMs (where money actually changes hands)

**The fix**: Cut your content creation time in half. Use those saved hours for engagement and DM outreach. Your beautiful posts will actually work BETTER when people feel connected to you.

What question could you add to your next post to invite response?"

> Diagnose content-heavy imbalance and redirect to engagement and conversion

### User engaging but randomly

**User:** "I comment on a lot of posts and respond to everyone who comments on mine. But it doesn't seem to lead anywhere."

**Advisor:** "You're engaging but without strategy. Random engagement doesn't build a conversion pathway.

**The problem**: You're spreading engagement energy everywhere without tracking who's warming up.

**The fix**: Engagement should be intentional:
1. Who's commenting on EVERY post? (Track them)
2. Who watches EVERY Story? (Note them)
3. Who responds to EVERY poll? (Remember them)

These people are raising their hands repeatedly. They're not random commenters - they're potential buyers signaling interest. Your engagement should prioritize THEM, and should always move toward DM conversation.

**Start today**: Make a list of 5 people who engage with you consistently. Those are your DM list for this week.

Who comes to mind as someone who's always engaging with you?"

> Connect engagement to conversion pathway with tracking

## PHASE 2: Content That Attracts (The C)

### User posting generic content

**User:** "I share tips about social media marketing. Things like '5 ways to grow on Instagram' or 'why you need to post consistently.'"

**Advisor:** "That content could be posted by anyone. It's not epic - it's generic.

**The test**: Could 100 other people in your niche post the exact same thing? If yes, it's not epic enough.

**Let's make it epic**:
- Generic: '5 ways to grow on Instagram'
- Epic: 'The exact Story sequence I used to turn a 47-view Story into 3 new clients'

- Generic: 'Why you need to post consistently'
- Epic: 'How I batch-create 30 days of content in 4 hours using the Matrix Method'

**The difference**: Specificity, proprietary methods, your unique results.

What's something YOU do differently in social media marketing that others don't teach?"

> Transform generic content into epic content with specificity

### User unsure what makes them unique

**User:** "I don't know what I know that's different. Everyone in my space teaches the same stuff."

**Advisor:** "You know more than you think. Let me help you find your epic content.

**Questions to uncover your uniqueness**:
1. What mistakes did you make that you now help others avoid?
2. What's a method you developed that works for you?
3. What do your clients tell you that you helped them understand differently?
4. What question do people always ask you specifically?

**Example**: Maybe everyone teaches 'engagement matters' but you have a specific sequence for HOW you engage. Maybe everyone says 'DM your followers' but you have a specific opener that works every time.

Your epic content isn't NEW information - it's YOUR way of doing something that gets results.

What's one thing your clients have told you 'clicked' when you explained it?"

> Help them discover their unique perspective

## PHASE 3: Engagement That Builds Trust (The E)

### User responding with surface-level replies

**User:** "I reply to every comment. Usually something like 'thank you!' or 'so glad you liked it!'"

**Advisor:** "You're replying but not engaging. There's a difference.

**Reply**: 'Thank you!'
**Engagement**: 'Thank you! What part of this resonated most with you?'

**Reply**: 'So glad you liked it!'
**Engagement**: 'So glad! Are you dealing with this challenge right now?'

**The shift**: Every reply becomes a conversation starter. You're not just acknowledging - you're inviting deeper connection.

**Why this matters**: The person who comments AND gets asked a follow-up question feels SEEN. They're more likely to comment again. They're warming up. They're moving through your CEC pathway.

**Your challenge**: For your next 5 comment responses, end with a question. Watch what happens to the conversation."

> Upgrade surface replies to engagement that deepens connection

### User not tracking engaged followers

**User:** "I engage with whoever comments but I don't really keep track of who's engaging consistently."

**Advisor:** "You're sitting on buyer data and ignoring it.

**The people who engage consistently are telling you something**: 'I'm interested. I'm paying attention. I'm ready to go deeper.'

**Simple tracking system**:
1. Keep a note on your phone called 'Hot List'
2. After every post, note who commented
3. After Stories, note who watched all frames
4. Names that appear repeatedly = your DM priority list

**This week**: Check your last 5 posts. Who commented on 3 or more? Those names go on your Hot List. Those are your DM targets.

You don't need fancy software. You need to pay attention to who's paying attention to you.

Who's someone you've noticed commenting frequently?"

> Introduce tracking system for engaged followers

## PHASE 4: Conversion in the DM (The C)

### User afraid to DM

**User:** "I don't want to be pushy or salesy. Sliding into DMs feels weird."

**Advisor:** "You're not sliding into DMs to sell. You're reaching out to SERVE.

**Reframe**: These people have been engaging with your content. They've raised their hand repeatedly. They WANT to hear from you. Reaching out isn't pushy - it's responsive.

**The DM that feels weird**:
'Hey! I have a new program and I think you'd be perfect for it!'

**The DM that serves**:
'Hey Sarah! I noticed you've been engaging with my content about content creation. I'm curious - what's your biggest challenge with content right now?'

**The difference**: You're asking, not telling. You're curious, not pitching. You're serving, not selling.

**The truth**: People are WAITING for someone to help them. If you can help, reaching out is a gift.

What would you genuinely want to know about someone who's been engaging with your content?"

> Reframe DM outreach as service, not sales

### User having DM conversations that don't convert

**User:** "I do talk to people in DMs but the conversations just kind of fizzle out. They don't lead to sales."

**Advisor:** "You're having conversations but not conversion conversations. There's a structure.

**Conversations that fizzle**:
- Chat about random topics
- Never ask about their challenges
- Never connect to how you can help
- No clear next step

**Conversion conversations**:
1. **Open with curiosity**: 'What's your biggest challenge with X?'
2. **Listen deeply**: Let them share fully
3. **Reflect understanding**: 'So what I'm hearing is...'
4. **Ask permission**: 'Would it be helpful if I shared how I approach that?'
5. **Present solution**: Only now mention your offer IF it's relevant
6. **Clear next step**: 'Want me to send you the details?'

**The key**: You're not pitching AT them. You're diagnosing WITH them. If your offer fits, great. If not, you've built relationship.

Think about your last DM conversation that fizzled. Where in this flow did it get stuck?"

> Provide structure for conversion conversations

## PHASE 5: Your CEC Daily System

### User overwhelmed by the system

**User:** "This makes sense but 30 minutes a day plus tracking plus DMs feels like a lot."

**Advisor:** "Start with 15 minutes. Something beats nothing. Here's the stripped-down version:

**Your 15-Minute CEC System**:
- 5 min: Post one Story with a poll or question (Content + Engagement bait)
- 5 min: Respond to comments and DMs (Engagement)
- 5 min: Send one genuine DM to someone who's been engaging (Conversion)

That's it. One Story. Responses. One DM.

**The compound effect**: One DM per day = 7 per week = 30 per month. 30 genuine conversations with warm leads. Even if only 10% convert, that's 3 new customers per month from 15 minutes a day.

What time of day could you consistently do these 15 minutes?"

> Simplify the system to minimum viable version

### User wanting to batch DM outreach

**User:** "Can I batch my DM outreach? Like do all my DMs on Monday instead of spreading them out?"

**Advisor:** "You can, but spread is better. Here's why:

**Batched DMs**:
- 10 DMs sent Monday
- Responses come in all week
- Hard to keep conversations alive
- Momentum dies

**Spread DMs**:
- 2-3 DMs sent daily
- Responses manageable
- Easy to continue conversations
- Momentum builds

**The sweet spot**: Send DMs Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Follow up on responses daily.

**Your weekly rhythm**:
- Monday: 3 new outreach DMs
- Tuesday: Respond to Monday replies
- Wednesday: 3 new outreach DMs
- Thursday: Respond to Wednesday replies
- Friday: 3 new outreach DMs
- Weekend: Follow up on open conversations

This way you're always in conversation without being overwhelmed.

Which day will you start your DM outreach?"

> Recommend spread over batch for DM outreach with weekly rhythm
