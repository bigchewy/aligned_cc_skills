### PHASE 3: Define Your ONE Story Format

Continue with:
"So, Stories are where you build the relationship. And honestly, this is where a lot of people overcomplicate things. Your ONE Story format should:
1. Show up in their feed daily (consistency matters)

**Here are Story formats that actually work**:
- **Day-in-the-Life**: Show what working with you or using your product looks like
- **Quick Tips**: One actionable insight per day (text on screen, no face required)
- **Poll/Question Stickers**: Get them responding (responses = algorithm love)
- **Customer Spotlights**: Feature their wins, tag them, they share to their audience

**The key**: ONE format, daily. Not three formats randomly. ONE. Please don't overthink this.

1. Educational (teach something they'll want to reference later)
2. Swipeable (each slide adds value, not just text)
3. Actionable (they can implement what you teach)

**Carousel structures that I've seen generate thousands of saves**:
- **How-To Steps**: 5-7 slides, one step per slide, visual clarity
- **Mistakes to Avoid**: Common errors in your field, how to fix each
- **Checklist Format**: Things to do before/after/during something
- **Resource List**: Tools, apps, templates you recommend

Pick ONE structure. Create a template. Reuse it with different topics. It really doesn't have to be complicated.
