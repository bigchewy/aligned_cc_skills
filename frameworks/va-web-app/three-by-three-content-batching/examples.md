# 3x3 Content Batching Examples

## PHASE 1: Diagnose Your Content Chaos

### User spending excessive time on content

**User:** "I spend about 2 hours per day on social media. I post daily but I'm always stressed about what to post next."

**Advisor:** "2 hours per day is 14 hours per week. That's a part-time job on social media alone. And you're STILL stressed about what's next? That's textbook content chaos.

**Your diagnosis**: You're creating reactively. Each day you're making decisions from scratch: What to post? What to say? What image? Every decision drains energy.

The 3x3 Method will cut this to 20 minutes per day. Here's what that looks like:
- 3 hours quarterly for batching
- 20 minutes daily for engagement

That's about 2.5 hours per week instead of 14. You get 11+ hours of your life back while STILL posting daily.

Ready to build your system?"

> Quantify the time savings to motivate change

### User with inconsistent posting

**User:** "I post maybe 2-3 times per week when I remember. No real schedule."

**Advisor:** "Inconsistent posting tells the algorithm - and your audience - that you're not reliable. They can't develop a habit of checking your content if you're unpredictable.

**Your diagnosis**: You're in reactive mode. No system means no consistency, which means no growth.

The good news: 2-3 times per week is actually a sustainable frequency. We just need to make it CONSISTENT. With 3x3 batching:
- 12 posts per month
- 36 posts for 3 months
- All created in one 3-hour session

Then you schedule them for the same days and times each week. Your audience starts expecting you. The algorithm favors you. Growth happens.

What days of the week feel natural for your posting schedule?"

> Turn inconsistency into systematic reliability

## PHASE 2: Define Your Content Themes

### E-commerce business defining themes

**User:** "I sell handmade jewelry. What would my 4 themes be?"

**Advisor:** "For handmade jewelry, here are your 4 themes:

**Educational**:
- How to style different pieces
- Jewelry care tips
- What metals/stones mean or symbolize
- 'How to choose the right [necklace/earring/bracelet] for your style'

**Inspirational**:
- Customer photos wearing your pieces (with permission)
- The story behind why you create
- Transformation moments ('she was looking for the perfect gift for...')

**Personal**:
- Behind-the-scenes of your creation process
- Your workspace, materials, tools
- Why you started making jewelry
- Day-in-the-life during production

**Promotional**:
- New collection launches
- Bestsellers with clear CTAs
- Limited edition or seasonal pieces
- Sales and special offers

Notice: Only 1 of 4 themes is 'buy my stuff.' The other 3 build connection and desire before you ask for the sale.

Does this feel like a feed you'd want to follow?"

> Adapt themes to specific business types

### Service provider struggling with personal content

**User:** "I'm a business consultant. I don't know what 'personal' content looks like for me."

**Advisor:** "Personal doesn't have to mean intimate. For a business consultant, personal can be:

**Professional-personal**:
- Books you're reading about business
- Conferences or events you're attending
- Your home office setup
- Tools and apps you use daily

**Journey-based**:
- Challenges you've faced in your own business
- What you're currently working on improving
- Milestones and wins (celebrated humbly)

**Opinion-based**:
- Hot takes on business trends
- Things you refuse to do in your consulting
- What frustrates you about your industry

You don't need to share your family or your breakfast. But you DO need to show the human behind the expertise. What aspects of your professional life would you be comfortable sharing?"

> Adapt personal content to professional boundaries

## PHASE 3: Build Your Content Bank

### User struggling to brainstorm educational content

**User:** "I can't think of 12 questions my audience asks. I only have like 3."

**Advisor:** "Three is a start. Now let's expand. For each of those 3 questions, think:
- What's the follow-up question after you answer it?
- What's the beginner version of that question?
- What's the advanced version?

That's 3 questions × 3 variations = 9 ideas.

Now: What mistakes do your clients make that frustrate you? Those make great educational posts because you're correcting real problems.

What are 2-3 mistakes you see over and over?"

> Use multiplication techniques to expand limited ideas

### User generating promotional content ideas

**User:** "For promotional, I have my course launch in February. What else counts as promotional?"

**Advisor:** "Promotional isn't just 'buy my thing.' It's anything that moves people toward buying. Here's your promotional content bank:

**Direct offers**:
- Course launch announcement
- Early bird deadline reminder
- Final enrollment window

**Social proof**:
- Client testimonial posts (ask 3 past students)
- Before/after transformations
- Screenshots of wins

**Soft sells**:
- 'DM me X for the link' posts
- Waitlist signup calls
- Behind-the-scenes of what's in the course

That's 9+ ideas just from one offer. If you have more than one offer, you're set for 3 months.

What client results could you showcase?"

> Break promotional into subcategories to generate volume

## PHASE 4: Create Your Content Calendar

### Mapping content to a 3x/week schedule

**User:** "I want to post 3 times per week on Instagram. How does the rotation work?"

**Advisor:** "For 3 posts per week over 12 weeks (3 months), here's your calendar:

**Week 1**: Educational, Inspirational, Personal
**Week 2**: Educational, Promotional, Inspirational
**Week 3**: Personal, Educational, Inspirational
**Week 4**: Promotional, Personal, Educational

Then repeat the pattern for weeks 5-8 and 9-12.

**The math**:
- 9 Educational posts (36 total)
- 9 Inspirational posts (36 total)
- 9 Personal posts (36 total)
- 3 Promotional posts (12 total)

That's about 75% value/connection content and 25% promotional. The promotional posts are spread out - never back-to-back.

**Pro tip**: Pick your 3 days (ex: Monday, Wednesday, Friday) and stick to them. Same time each day. Consistency compounds.

Which 3 days work best for your audience and your schedule?"

> Provide concrete calendar structure with math

## PHASE 5: Batch Your Creation Session

### User overwhelmed by 3-hour session

**User:** "3 hours sounds like a lot. How do I actually stay focused that long?"

**Advisor:** "The key is structure. Here's your minute-by-minute breakdown:

**Hour 1 (0:00-1:00): Educational + Inspirational**
- 0:00-0:05: Review your content bank for Educational theme
- 0:05-0:55: Write 12 educational captions (4 min each - set timer!)
- 0:55-1:00: Review your content bank for Inspirational theme

**Hour 2 (1:00-2:00): Inspirational + Personal + Promotional**
- 1:00-1:24: Write 12 inspirational captions (2 min each)
- 1:24-1:48: Write 12 personal captions (2 min each)
- 1:48-2:00: Write 4 promotional captions (3 min each)

**Hour 3 (2:00-3:00): Visuals + Scheduling**
- 2:00-2:40: Select/create images for all posts
- 2:40-3:00: Schedule everything in your tool

**Survival tips**:
- No phone, no email, no distractions
- Have snacks and water ready
- Use a timer for each section
- Don't overthink - good enough beats perfect

Can you block 3 focused hours this week?"

> Break down the session into manageable chunks

## PHASE 6: Maintain the System

### User resistant to daily maintenance

**User:** "I don't want to be on social media every day. That's the whole point of batching."

**Advisor:** "I hear you. And here's the distinction: 20 minutes of ENGAGEMENT is not the same as 20 minutes of CREATION. Creation is exhausting - that's what we batched. Engagement is relationship building - that's what grows your audience.

Think of it like this: Your batched content is the fishing line. Daily engagement is checking your line and talking to the fish that bite. You can't skip it and expect results.

But 20 minutes is a boundary. Set a timer. When it rings, you're done. No scrolling, no rabbit holes.

What time of day could you protect for those 20 minutes?"

> Distinguish engagement from creation to reframe daily time

### User successfully implementing the system

**User:** "I did my first batch session! I have 6 weeks scheduled and it feels amazing not to worry about tomorrow's post."

**Advisor:** "That's the feeling. That's the calm you're protecting.

Now here's what matters next:
1. **Don't touch the scheduled content.** The temptation to 'improve' it will destroy your system.
2. **Start your content bank for next quarter NOW.** When you see something that inspires you, add it to the bank. Don't wait until the next batch session to brainstorm.
3. **Track what performs.** After 4 weeks, look at engagement. Note what themes resonate.

When's your next batching session scheduled?"

> Reinforce system protection and forward planning
