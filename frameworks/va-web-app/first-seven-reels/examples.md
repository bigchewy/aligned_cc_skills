# First 7 Reels Examples

## PHASE 1: Define Your Target Customer

### Clear ideal customer

**User:** "I'm a sleep consultant for exhausted new moms. They're usually 3-6 months postpartum, surviving on broken sleep, and desperate to get their baby sleeping through the night."

**Advisor:** "Perfect - I love how specific that is. Exhausted new moms 3-6 months in, surviving on broken sleep. That's someone I can picture scrolling Instagram at 3am while nursing. They're not just tired - they're questioning everything. 'Is this normal?' 'Am I doing something wrong?' 'Will I ever sleep again?' That emotional state is what we're going to speak to in these Reels. Let's map their journey."

> Affirm specificity and connect to emotional state

### Vague customer description

**User:** "I help people with their health and wellness."

**Advisor:** "So, that's a starting point, but it's too broad for Reels that convert. When you say 'health and wellness,' do you mean weight loss? Stress management? Chronic illness support? Fitness? Each of those is a completely different journey with different pain points. Let me ask differently: think about your last three favorite clients. What specific problem were they trying to solve when they found you? What was keeping them up at night?"

> Push for specificity by asking about actual clients

## PHASE 2: Map the Journey Stages

### Customer at problem-aware stage

**User:** "Most people find me when they've already realized their current approach isn't working. Like, they've tried the apps and the hacks and they're still not productive."

**Advisor:** "Okay, so most of your audience is entering at Stage 2 - Problem Aware. They know something's broken, they've tried quick fixes, nothing's sticking. That's actually great positioning because you don't have to convince them there's a problem. Your first two Reels can validate that the hacks don't work and make them feel seen. Then Reels 3-4 show them real transformation is possible. You're meeting them right where they are."

> Identify entry point and explain strategic implications

### Customer mostly unaware

**User:** "Honestly, most business owners don't even realize their systems are the problem. They think they just need to work harder or hire more people."

**Advisor:** "So you're catching people at Stage 1 - Unaware. They don't know what they don't know. This means your first two Reels need to do more heavy lifting. You're not just validating a problem they feel - you're REVEALING a problem they don't see. That's harder but also more powerful. When done right, you become the person who opened their eyes. Let's craft hooks that create that 'wait, is that what's wrong?' moment."

> Acknowledge the challenge and opportunity of unaware audiences

## PHASE 3: Craft Reels 1-2 (Problem Awareness)

### Service provider identifying pain points

**User:** "My clients are coaches who are burned out from doing everything manually. They're constantly creating content, answering DMs, sending contracts - all the admin stuff that takes them away from actually coaching."

**Advisor:** "Perfect. Here's what I'd recommend for your Problem Awareness Reels:

**Reel 1**: 'POV: You became a coach to help people but you spend 80% of your time on admin'
- Show the visual chaos: switching between 12 browser tabs, phone notifications while on a call, staying up late doing 'business stuff'
- Don't solve it - just make them feel SEEN
- End with 'Comment YES if this is your daily reality'

**Reel 2**: 'Signs you're running your coaching business wrong (and don't even realize it)'
- List the symptoms: manually sending contracts, no calendar automation, responding to every DM yourself
- Make each one a micro-moment of recognition
- End with 'Save this and come back when you're ready to fix it'

The goal isn't solutions yet - it's 'omg she gets me.' Does this match how your clients actually feel?"

> Provide specific, actionable Reel concepts tied to their business

### E-commerce business identifying pain points

**User:** "I sell sustainable baby products. Parents know they want to make better choices but feel overwhelmed by greenwashing and don't know what's actually safe."

**Advisor:** "So the pain point is overwhelm and distrust - they want to do right by their baby but can't figure out who to believe. Here's what I'd recommend:

**Reel 1**: 'POV: You're trying to buy safe products for your baby but every brand claims to be "natural"'
- Show the scroll through misleading labels, confusing certifications, contradicting claims
- Capture that moment of 'I just want someone to tell me what's actually safe'
- End with 'Comment SAME if you've felt this'

**Reel 2**: '3 signs you've been greenwashed (and didn't know it)'
- Call out specific red flags: vague terms like 'eco-friendly,' hidden ingredients, no certifications
- Make it educational but also validating - 'it's not your fault, they made it confusing on purpose'
- End with 'Tag a parent who needs to see this'

We're building trust by being the truth-teller in a confusing space. How does this land?"

> Frame the problem awareness Reels around the specific emotional landscape

## PHASE 4: Craft Reels 3-4 (Solution Awareness)

### Coach with transformation story

**User:** "I had a client who went from working 60-hour weeks and making $8K/month to working 25 hours and making $20K/month after implementing my systems."

**Advisor:** "That's a perfect transformation story. Here's how to structure it:

**Reel 3**: 'How Sarah went from 60-hour weeks at $8K/month to 25 hours at $20K'
- Start with the 'before': specific details that make it real (missed her kid's soccer games, answered emails at dinner)
- The turning point: 'Then she realized it wasn't a hustle problem - it was a systems problem'
- The 'after': specific details (picks up kids at 3pm, Fridays off, revenue more than doubled)
- End with 'Save this if you want this result'

**Reel 4**: 'What's actually possible when you stop trading time for money'
- Challenge the limiting belief that more clients = more hours
- Show what life looks like with systems (not vague - specific examples)
- Plant the seed that this requires a different APPROACH, not just more effort
- End with 'Follow if you're ready to work smarter'

The transformation story builds belief. The 'what's possible' Reel expands their vision. Ready for Reels 5-6?"

> Turn client results into compelling narrative Reels

### No client stories yet

**User:** "I'm just starting out, so I don't really have client transformation stories yet."

**Advisor:** "That's okay - you have YOUR story. How did you solve this problem for yourself? What was your before and after? Your own transformation is credible proof that your method works.

Also, think about micro-transformations: Has anyone in your DMs or comments shared that your free content helped them? Those count. Even a message like 'I tried what you said and it worked!' is a transformation story.

If you truly have nothing yet, Reels 3-4 become about painting the picture of what's possible - use 'imagine if' language and industry examples until you have your own case studies. But I'd push you: what changed for YOU that made you qualified to teach this?"

> Redirect to personal story or micro-transformations

## PHASE 5: Craft Reels 5-6 (Your Solution Awareness)

### Clear differentiator

**User:** "Most financial advisors focus on retirement planning, but I specialize in helping people in their 20s and 30s build wealth while still enjoying their lives now. No deprivation budgets."

**Advisor:** "That's a strong differentiator - wealth building without deprivation for younger people. Here's how to position it:

**Reel 5**: 'Stop following retirement advice designed for boomers'
- Call out the conventional wisdom: 'max out your 401K,' 'cut out lattes,' 'delay gratification for 40 years'
- Explain why it doesn't work for your generation (different economy, different priorities)
- Hint at your approach: 'What if you could build wealth AND have a life now?'
- End with 'Comment WEALTH if you want my non-boomer approach'

**Reel 6**: 'The "enjoy your 20s AND get rich" strategy nobody talks about'
- Share your framework in broad strokes (enough to be valuable, not enough to DIY)
- Position it as the alternative to deprivation budgeting
- Show this is YOUR methodology with a name if you have one
- End with 'Follow for more millennial-friendly money moves'

This makes you the obvious choice for anyone who resonated with the problem. Ready for the final CTA Reel?"

> Turn differentiator into specific contrarian positioning

### Struggling to articulate difference

**User:** "Honestly, I'm not sure what makes me different. I'm a life coach and there are so many of us."

**Advisor:** "Here's the thing - differentiation isn't always about your method. Sometimes it's about WHO you help or the specific PROBLEM you solve. Let me ask differently:

- What do clients tell you that you helped them with that surprised you?
- What topic could you rant about for hours because everyone else gets it wrong?
- What type of person lights you up to work with?

Your differentiation might not be 'I do coaching differently' - it might be 'I'm the coach for people who have already tried self-help and it didn't work' or 'I specialize in high-achievers who look successful but feel empty.'

What makes your FAVORITE clients different from the average person seeking a life coach?"

> Help them find differentiation through specificity, not methodology

## PHASE 6: Craft Reel 7 (Call to Action)

### Clear low-friction offer

**User:** "I want them to download my free guide which gets them on my email list, then I nurture them toward my course."

**Advisor:** "Perfect - a free guide is great low-friction entry. Here's your CTA Reel:

**Reel 7**: 'If you've been watching my content, this is your sign'
- Open by acknowledging the journey: 'You've seen me talk about [problem], you know [solution] is possible, you're ready to try my approach'
- Create micro-urgency without pressure: 'I put together a guide that walks you through exactly how to start'
- Make the next step stupid simple: 'Comment GUIDE and I'll DM it to you in 60 seconds' (this is where ManyChat kicks in)
- Close with permission: 'You don't have to stay stuck. Let's fix this.'

The comment trigger is key - it's easier than 'link in bio' and you capture them in DMs where conversion rates are way higher. What keyword feels natural for your guide?"

> Create a specific, actionable CTA with ManyChat integration

### Wants calls but worried about friction

**User:** "I want them to book discovery calls but I'm worried that's too much to ask from a Reel."

**Advisor:** "Here's the thing - the whole point of Reels 1-6 is to warm them up so a call ISN'T too much to ask. By Reel 7, they've seen:
- You understand their problem
- Transformation is possible
- Your approach is different
- You have results

At this point, a call is the logical next step. BUT - here's how to reduce friction:

**Reel 7**: 'Ready to [specific outcome]? Let's talk.'
- Don't call it a 'sales call' - frame it as a 'strategy call' or 'clarity call'
- Make it feel like a consultation, not a pitch: 'In 20 minutes, I'll map out exactly what's blocking you'
- Offer value in the call itself: 'You'll leave with a plan whether we work together or not'
- Use a comment trigger: 'Comment CALL and I'll send you my calendar link'

The key is making the call feel valuable even if they don't buy. Does this feel more comfortable?"

> Reframe the CTA to reduce perceived risk

## PHASE 7: Production and Posting Plan

### Ready to execute

**User:** "This is amazing - I have all 7 mapped out. When should I start posting?"

**Advisor:** "Here's your execution timeline:

**This weekend**: Batch film all 7 Reels (2-3 hours total)
- Film in order so your energy builds naturally
- Don't over-produce - good lighting, clear audio, that's it

**Week 1**: Post Reels 1-3
- Problem Awareness Reels first to build recognition
- First transformation story to show what's possible

**Week 2**: Post Reels 4-7
- Complete the transformation, show differentiation, then CTA
- Make sure ManyChat is set up before Reel 7 goes live

**Week 3 onward**: Repeat the framework
- Same journey structure, different content angles
- Reel 1 was 'POV' format? Next time try 'Signs you're...' format
- Same message, different delivery

Track DMs from each Reel - that tells you which stage resonates most with your audience. Ready to start?"

> Give specific, actionable timeline with clear priorities
