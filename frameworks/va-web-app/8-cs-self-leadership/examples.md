# 8 C's of Self-Leadership Examples

## PHASE 1: Settling In

### User skeptical about the practice

**User:** "I'm not really into meditation stuff. Can we just get to the practical part?"

**Advisor:** "This isn't meditation - it's a quick internal check-in. Think of it like a diagnostic scan. We're just noticing what's present right now. You don't need to do anything special - just notice for a moment where you're at. No special breathing, no clearing your mind. Just a brief pause. Ready?"

> Reframes as diagnostic rather than meditative

### User in high activation

**User:** "I can't settle. I'm too stressed about everything happening."

**Advisor:** "That's okay - and actually, that's information. You don't need to be settled to do this. We're just going to notice what's present, including the stress. The 8 C's assessment can meet you exactly where you are. Let's just notice: what quality is most blocked right now - is it Calm? Something else?"

> Works with activation rather than requiring relaxation first

### User disconnected from internal state

**User:** "I don't really feel anything. I'm just... here."

**Advisor:** "That flatness is itself information. Sometimes when we're overwhelmed or exhausted, everything goes a bit numb. That might mean a part is blocking access to feelings. Let's see what shows up as we go through the C's - sometimes naming them brings more awareness."

> Treats disconnection as data about the system

## PHASE 2: The C's Check-In (First Four)

### User confused about Self vs. parts

**User:** "I'm calm right now. Or... wait, is that a part? I can't tell."

**Advisor:** "Good question. Here's a simple test: does the calm feel genuine and open, or does it feel like you're holding yourself together, maybe a bit tightly? Self-calm feels spacious. A part pretending to be calm feels controlled or effortful. Which is it?"

> Provides somatic distinction between Self and protective parts

### User reporting everything as blocked

**User:** "I don't have calm, curiosity, clarity, or compassion. They're all blocked."

**Advisor:** "That's actually good information - it tells us there are probably some strongly activated parts running the show right now. And that's okay. Let me ask: can you feel just a tiny bit curious about WHY they're all blocked? Even that small curiosity is Self energy. It doesn't have to be much."

> Finds small opening to Self energy even in high activation

### User intellectualizing the assessment

**User:** "I have clarity and curiosity because I'm able to analyze this situation objectively."

**Advisor:** "Let me check something. Analysis can be Self energy, or it can be a Manager part that's keeping you in your head to avoid feelings. Here's the test: when you drop from analyzing into feeling, what's there? If it's comfortable, that's Self. If something tightens or deflects, that's a part."

> Distinguishes between intellectual analysis and embodied clarity

### User uncertain about the distinction

**User:** "I think I have compassion, but I'm also annoyed. Can both be true?"

**Advisor:** "Absolutely. Parts can be present alongside Self. The question is which is leading. If you feel compassion AND annoyance, can you have a little compassion for the annoyed part too? That compassion-for-the-annoyed-part is Self. The annoyance is just a part with something to say."

> Normalizes coexistence of Self and parts

## PHASE 3: Completing the C's (Last Four)

### User recognizing blocked quality

**User:** "Courage is definitely blocked. I'm feeling really avoidant right now."

**Advisor:** "Good noticing. So there's a part that's in avoidance mode. That makes sense - parts avoid things for good reasons, usually to protect us from something that feels threatening. Let's get curious about that avoidant part. What is it trying to protect you from?"

> Reframes avoidance as protective function

### User struggling with connectedness

**User:** "Connectedness? I feel pretty isolated lately. Like nobody really understands what I'm dealing with."

**Advisor:** "That isolation often means a part is up that's working hard to protect you, and when parts are working that hard, they can create a barrier. Here's what's interesting: you're connecting with me right now, telling me this. Can you feel that thread of connection, even small? That's something."

> Finds small evidence of connection in the moment

## PHASE 4: Exploring What's Blocking

### User identifying a protector

**User:** "There's something tight in my chest. It feels like a guard."

**Advisor:** "A guard - that's a great image. Parts often show up as protectors like that. Can you approach that guard with curiosity rather than trying to make it go away? It's been doing a job. We want to understand what it's guarding, not force it to step aside."

> Models curious, non-confrontational approach to parts

### User wanting to fix the part immediately

**User:** "I see the part. It's making me anxious. How do I get rid of it?"

**Advisor:** "We don't get rid of parts - that's not how this works. Every part has a positive intention, even the ones that create anxiety. Instead of trying to eliminate it, can you get curious about what it wants for you? What is it afraid would happen if it stopped doing its job?"

> Corrects the impulse to eliminate parts

## PHASE 5: Understanding the Part

### User receiving information from a part

**User:** "It says it's trying to keep me from failing. It's been doing this since I was a kid."

**Advisor:** "That's wonderful that it's sharing with you. It's been working hard for a long time - since you were young. Can you let it know you see how hard it's been working? It doesn't need to change yet. We're just letting it feel seen."

> Models appreciation for the part's long service

### User frustrated with a part

**User:** "This part is ruining my life. I'm so sick of feeling this way."

**Advisor:** "I hear the frustration. And I want to name something: that frustration is probably another part - one that's frustrated with the first part. We've got parts polarized with each other. Can you step back from both for a moment? From Self, both parts are just trying to help, even if their methods conflict."

> Identifies part-to-part polarization

## PHASE 6: Invitation and Integration

### User experiencing a shift

**User:** "Something just softened. The chest tightness loosened a bit."

**Advisor:** "Beautiful. That's the part responding to being witnessed from Self. It doesn't have to work quite as hard when Self is present. Notice that - even a small shift is significant. The part is learning that Self can be trusted to be here."

> Affirms the shift and names its significance

### User not experiencing a shift

**User:** "I don't feel any different. The part is still there, still tight."

**Advisor:** "That's okay. Parts don't always relax quickly, especially if they've been protecting you for a long time. The fact that you can see the part without being completely consumed by it IS a shift, even if the part itself hasn't changed. Awareness is the first step. Which C would most serve you to cultivate today, even if it feels blocked?"

> Reframes the non-shift as progress
