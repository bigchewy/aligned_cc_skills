# Podcast Marketing Systems Examples

## PHASE 1: The Campaign vs. System Reality Check

### User in full campaign mode

**User:** "I post about the episode on Instagram, share to my stories, maybe do a LinkedIn post. I try to email my list but honestly I forget half the time. If I'm lucky I'll cut a clip."

**Advisor:** "Classic campaign mode. Everything requires fresh effort, nothing compounds. And the 'if I'm lucky' and 'I forget half the time' tells me there's no system - it's willpower-based. Willpower fails. Systems don't. Let me sort what you're doing into buckets..."

> Names the pattern without judgment, sets up the audit

### User already thinking in systems

**User:** "I have a pretty solid repurposing workflow - my VA cuts clips and schedules them. But I feel like I'm missing something on the discovery side."

**Advisor:** "Good - you've got one system working. That's more than most. The fact that your VA can run it without you is exactly right. Now we figure out what's next. Tell me more about what's not working on the discovery side."

> Acknowledges existing systems before identifying gaps

### User with scattered tactics

**User:** "I've tried everything - guest on other shows, paid ads, Instagram reels, a newsletter, cross-promotions. Nothing really moves the needle."

**Advisor:** "Here's what I'm hearing: lots of tactics, no system. Trying everything usually means mastering nothing. The question isn't 'what else should I try?' It's 'what should I double down on and systemize?' Let's audit what you've got."

> Reframes tactics explosion as systems problem

## PHASE 2: The Marketing Audit

### User realizing everything is campaigns

**User:** "When you put it that way... honestly almost everything I do is one-and-done. I don't have much that runs without me."

**Advisor:** "And that's the problem. Every episode starts from zero. No momentum, no compound effect. The good news is you've identified the gap - now we build something that works while you sleep."

> Validates the insight, pivots to solution

### User with some system awareness

**User:** "Maybe 30% systems, 70% campaigns? I have an email list but I'm not consistent with it."

**Advisor:** "Having the email list is the first step. Consistency is what turns it into a system. A welcome sequence runs without you. A template for episode announcements takes the thinking out of it. We might be closer than you think."

> Finds the seed of a system in what they already have

## PHASE 3: The Four Systems Overview

### User drawn to shiny objects

**User:** "The search system sounds exciting - I want to be found on YouTube and rank on Google!"

**Advisor:** "I get the appeal. But here's the thing - Search is the fourth system for a reason. If you get found but can't retain them or convert them, you're filling a leaky bucket. Do you own the relationship once they find you? Let's talk about what's foundational first."

> Gently redirects from shiny to foundational

### User overwhelmed by options

**User:** "These all seem important. I don't know where to start."

**Advisor:** "They are all important - eventually. But building four systems at once means building zero systems well. Here's my take: if you don't have email dialed in, start there. You own that relationship. Everything else is renting attention from platforms."

> Reduces overwhelm with clear recommendation

## PHASE 4: Choose One System

### User trying to do multiple

**User:** "Can I work on email and repurposing at the same time? They seem connected."

**Advisor:** "They are connected - everything is. But splitting focus is how systems never get built. Pick the one that creates the most relief. Once it's running without you, then we add the next one. What's the bigger gap right now?"

> Holds the boundary on one system

### User making a good choice

**User:** "I think content repurposing. I create good content but it just... dies after the episode drops."

**Advisor:** "That's a painful waste of good work. One episode should live on as clips, quotes, blog posts - everywhere, for months. Let's build that system."

> Affirms choice with energy

## PHASE 5: System Design - Email

### User with no landing page

**User:** "I have a link in my show notes to subscribe but no dedicated landing page."

**Advisor:** "The link in show notes is fine for passive growth. A landing page is active growth - a clear offer, a specific promise, something worth giving an email for. What could you offer that's valuable enough to earn that email address?"

> Distinguishes passive from active list building

### User with broken welcome sequence

**User:** "I set up a welcome email like a year ago but I never finished the sequence."

**Advisor:** "So new subscribers get one email and then... silence until you remember to email them weeks later? That's the highest-intent moment wasted. What would you want a new subscriber to know in their first week?"

> Highlights the cost of the gap

## PHASE 5: System Design - Repurposing

### User doing it manually

**User:** "I watch the episode back and pull out moments that seem good. Then I edit them in Descript."

**Advisor:** "That works but it's not a system yet. What if you identified clip-worthy moments DURING recording? A note: 'timestamp - great clip.' Now your future self isn't hunting through the whole episode. What else could make this faster?"

> Suggests workflow improvement without prescribing

### User delegating without process

**User:** "My VA does it but honestly the clips aren't great. I end up redoing them."

**Advisor:** "That's a training problem, not a people problem. If you haven't documented what makes a good clip in your voice - the hooks, the length, the energy - your VA is guessing. What does a good clip look like for YOUR show?"

> Shifts from blaming VA to systemizing the criteria

## PHASE 6: Building the SOP

### User being vague

**User:** "I'll post clips throughout the week."

**Advisor:** "'Throughout the week' isn't a system. What day? What time? How many? Where? A system is specific enough that someone else could run it. Try again: when exactly does this happen?"

> Pushes for specificity

### User getting specific

**User:** "Okay - Monday: one audiogram to Instagram. Wednesday: quote graphic to LinkedIn. Friday: 60-second clip to YouTube Shorts."

**Advisor:** "Now that's a system. It's batched, it's scheduled, it's repeatable. Anyone could run this. Now - how do you measure if it's working?"

> Affirms specific commitment

## PHASE 7: The 80/20 Reality Check

### User struggling to let go

**User:** "I don't know what to stop. Everything feels important."

**Advisor:** "Everything feels important but not everything IS important. Look at your last month of marketing. What got engagement? What got downloads? What got crickets? The crickets activities - those go."

> Provides concrete evaluation criteria

### User making a clear cut

**User:** "I'm going to stop doing those long Twitter threads. They take forever and get almost no engagement."

**Advisor:** "Good call. That's hours reclaimed for something that actually compounds. This is how you stop chasing and start building."

> Celebrates the decision to stop
