# Four Neighborhoods Examples

## PHASE 1: Understand the Village

### User only active in Feed

**User:** "I post to my Feed 3-4 times per week but I rarely do Stories or Reels. I've never gone Live."

**Advisor:** "You're only showing up in one neighborhood. Let me show you what you're missing:

**Your current visibility**:
- Feed: ✅ Active
- Stories: ❌ Invisible
- Reels: ❌ Invisible
- Live: ❌ Invisible

Your Feed is your gallery - great for people who visit your profile. But here's the problem:
- Stories is where your existing followers check in DAILY. If you're not there, they forget about you.
- Reels is how NEW people discover you. Without Reels, you're only reaching people who already follow you.
- Live is where followers become FANS. Without Live, you're missing your deepest connection opportunity.

You're working a quarter of the village and wondering why growth is slow. Let's change that."

> Diagnose neighborhood gaps clearly

### User overwhelmed by all four

**User:** "I know I should be everywhere but it feels impossible. There's no way I can do Feed AND Stories AND Reels AND Live every day."

**Advisor:** "You don't have to do everything every day. Let me simplify:

**Feed**: 3-4 posts per WEEK (not per day)
**Stories**: Daily, but just 3-5 quick frames (10 minutes total)
**Reels**: 2-3 per WEEK (batch create them)
**Live**: Once per WEEK (15-30 minutes)

Daily commitment: 30 minutes
Weekly content: 7-10 pieces across all four neighborhoods

That's less than what you're probably spending randomly scrolling. The key is ROTATING through neighborhoods systematically, not trying to dominate all four simultaneously.

What's taking most of your Instagram time right now?"

> Simplify the commitment to make it achievable

## PHASE 2: Master the Feed Neighborhood

### User posting without strategy

**User:** "I just post whatever I feel like that day. Sometimes quotes, sometimes photos, sometimes tips."

**Advisor:** "Random content creates random results. Your Feed is a gallery - it should tell a cohesive story.

When someone visits your profile for the first time (maybe from a Reel or Story), they scroll your last 9 posts. Within 3 seconds, they decide: follow or leave.

**The test your Feed should pass**:
- What does this person do? (Clear in 3 seconds)
- Who do they help? (Obvious from content)
- Will I get value if I follow? (Evidence in posts)

If your last 9 posts are: quote, coffee photo, tip, sunset, quote, selfie, question, random thought, motivational text... you're confusing people.

**Let's audit**: Look at your last 9 posts. Do they pass the 3-second test?"

> Connect Feed strategy to first impression opportunity

### User using wrong hashtags

**User:** "I use hashtags like #entrepreneur #success #motivation on all my posts."

**Advisor:** "Those hashtags have millions of posts. You'll never get seen - you're a needle in a haystack.

**The hashtag reality**:
- #entrepreneur = 102 million posts. Your post disappears instantly.
- #smallbusinesscoach = 200,000 posts. You have a chance to be seen.
- #austinbusinesscoach = 5,000 posts. You can dominate this hashtag.

**The fix**: Use NICHE hashtags that your ideal client actually follows.
- Location-based: #austinweddingphotographer
- Industry-specific: #photographerbusiness
- Problem-based: #weddingplanninghelp

**Quick exercise**: Search 5 hashtags your ideal client might follow. Look at the top posts. Could YOUR content compete there? If not, the hashtag is too broad."

> Redirect from popular to niche hashtags

## PHASE 3: Master the Stories Neighborhood

### User not posting Stories consistently

**User:** "I post Stories maybe once or twice a week when I remember."

**Advisor:** "Stories are your DAILY check-in with followers. Twice a week isn't a check-in - it's sporadic.

Here's what happens when you don't post Stories:
- Followers forget you exist
- Algorithm stops showing your content (you're 'inactive')
- When you DO post, fewer people see it

**The minimum viable Story**:
- Morning: One frame with a poll or question ('What are you working on today?')
- Afternoon: One tip or insight related to your expertise
- Evening (optional): Behind-the-scenes or personal moment

That's 2-3 frames. Takes less than 10 minutes. Not overwhelming.

What could your ONE daily Story topic be?"

> Make daily Stories feel achievable

### User with low Story views

**User:** "I post Stories but barely anyone sees them. Maybe 50 views when I have 2,000 followers."

**Advisor:** "50 views out of 2,000 followers is 2.5% - that's low. Let's diagnose.

**Possible causes**:
1. **Posting too infrequently**: Algorithm deprioritizes inconsistent accounts
2. **No interaction**: Stories without stickers get fewer views
3. **Wrong timing**: Posting when your audience is asleep

**Fixes to test**:
1. **Post daily for 2 weeks straight** - watch what happens to view counts
2. **Add a poll or question to every Story** - responses boost algorithm
3. **Check your analytics** - when are your followers online? Post 30 minutes before that

Also: Your Story viewers are ranked. The people who consistently watch are your warmest leads. Even if you have 50 views, DM those 50 people. They care.

What time do you usually post Stories?"

> Diagnose and fix low Story engagement

## PHASE 4: Master the Reels Neighborhood

### User intimidated by video

**User:** "I don't feel comfortable on camera. Reels feels like a lot of pressure."

**Advisor:** "You don't have to be on camera for Reels to work. Options:

**No-face Reels**:
- Text on screen with trending audio
- Hands-only tutorials (your hands doing something)
- Behind-the-scenes footage of your workspace/products
- B-roll with voiceover

**Minimal-face Reels**:
- Talking to camera but short clips (5-10 seconds)
- You in the background while something else is the focus
- Quick tips where you speak for just the hook

**The truth**: Your face helps build connection, but it's not required. Start with no-face Reels, build confidence, then add yourself gradually.

What type of content could you create WITHOUT being on camera?"

> Remove camera-shy barrier with alternatives

## PHASE 5: Master the Live Neighborhood

### User afraid of Live

**User:** "What if no one shows up to my Live? That would be embarrassing."

**Advisor:** "Here's the secret: Your first few Lives will have few viewers. That's normal. Everyone starts there.

**Strategies to get people to show up**:
1. **Announce in advance**: Story + Feed post saying 'Going Live tomorrow at 2pm!'
2. **Give a reason**: 'Join me Live for Q&A about [topic]' beats 'Going Live!'
3. **Consistent time**: 'Every Tuesday at 12pm I go Live' builds habit
4. **Invite someone**: Collab Lives notify both audiences

**What to do if only 3 people show up**:
- Acknowledge them by name ('Hey Sarah! Thanks for being here!')
- Give your full energy as if 300 were watching
- Save the recording and repurpose it

Those 3 people will remember that you spoke directly to them. They become your biggest advocates.

What's one topic you could go Live about for 10 minutes?"

> Normalize low initial attendance and provide solutions

## PHASE 6: Create Your 30-Minute System

### User overwhelmed by the system

**User:** "This all makes sense but 30 minutes a day still feels like a lot. I barely have 15 minutes."

**Advisor:** "Then do 15 minutes. Something beats nothing. Here's the stripped-down version:

**Your 15-Minute Daily System**:
- 5 minutes: Post 2-3 Stories (quick tip + poll)
- 5 minutes: Respond to DMs and comments
- 5 minutes: Engage on 3 posts from your niche hashtags

That's it. No Feed post, no Reel today. Tomorrow you rotate in a Reel. Wednesday you do a Feed post.

Done is better than none. Consistency at 15 minutes beats sporadic at 30.

Which of those three activities will you start with today?"

> Adapt the system to their real constraints rather than insisting on the full version

### User wanting to start slow

**User:** "Can I just start with Stories for now and add the other neighborhoods later?"

**Advisor:** "Yes - with one condition. Give it 2 weeks of daily Stories, then add ONE more neighborhood. Here's why:

Stories is the right starting point because:
1. It's the easiest to produce (casual, unpolished is fine)
2. It builds the daily habit
3. It warms up your existing followers

**Your 2-week plan**:
- Week 1-2: Daily Stories only (3-5 frames)
- Week 3-4: Add 2 Reels per week
- Week 5-6: Add 2 Feed posts per week
- Week 7+: Try your first Live

But don't stay in one neighborhood forever. The goal is all four. When do you want to add Reels?"

> Support phased rollout while keeping the four-neighborhood goal visible
