# 1-1-1-1 Method Examples

## PHASE 1: Define Your Revenue Goal

### User with vague goal

**User:** "I want to grow my Instagram and get more visibility for my coaching business."

**Advisor:** "So, visibility is great, but I need to push back a little. Visibility doesn't pay the bills. What I care about is: what specific action do you want someone to take after seeing your content? Book a discovery call? Download your lead magnet? Buy a digital product? Because 'grow my Instagram' isn't a revenue goal - it's a vanity goal. And honestly, I've seen people with 500 followers booking clients while people with 50,000 followers are broke. What's the ONE action you want people to take?"

> Redirect vague visibility goals to specific revenue actions

### User focused on followers

**User:** "I want to hit 10,000 followers so I can get the swipe-up feature."

**Advisor:** "I'm going to stop you right there. The swipe-up feature isn't even a thing anymore - everyone gets link stickers now. But more importantly, 10K followers is a vanity metric. I have students with under 1,000 followers generating $5K months from Instagram. So let me ask differently: what would getting those followers DO for your business? Are you trying to book calls? Sell a course? Get people on your email list? That's what we're building toward."

> Challenge follower-count goals by refocusing on revenue outcome

### User with clear revenue goal

**User:** "I want people to book discovery calls for my design services. I charge $3,500 per project."

**Advisor:** "Perfect. Now we're talking about revenue. So at $3,500 per project, you need what - two clients a month to hit $7K? Three for $10K? That's doable with a tiny audience if your system converts. Everything we build is going to funnel toward getting people to book that discovery call. That's the ONLY metric that matters."

> Affirm clear goals and connect to revenue math

## PHASE 2: Find Your ONE Reel Style

### Service business identifying their style

**User:** "I'm a wedding photographer targeting couples getting married in the next 12 months."

**Advisor:** "For wedding photography, your ONE Reel style should be **Before-After Transformation**. Here's why:

Engaged couples are scrolling Instagram imagining their wedding day. They want to SEE what their photos could look like. Show them:
- Nervous couple arriving → relaxed couple laughing during portraits
- Empty venue → decorated venue with couple in center
- Candid chaos behind scenes → final stunning image

This format does three things: shows your skill, creates emotional connection, and positions you as the person who captures magic. Don't try day-in-the-life vlogs, don't try educational content about photography. Transformation. Every. Single. Time.

Does this feel like something you can create from your existing work?"

> Match the format to what the audience is actually seeking

### Coach struggling to choose

**User:** "I'm a business coach but I feel like I should be doing educational Reels AND behind-the-scenes AND testimonials."

**Advisor:** "Stop. That thinking is exactly why you're overwhelmed. You don't need three formats - you need ONE format that works, repeated until you're sick of it.

Here's the truth: your audience doesn't see all your content. They see maybe 10%. What feels repetitive to you feels fresh to them.

For a business coach, I'd recommend **Problem-Solution** Reels. Show the specific chaos your ideal clients face (overwhelm, inconsistent revenue, burnout), then hint at your framework without giving everything away. 'If this sounds familiar, comment HELP and I'll send you my first step.'

Educational content makes you a teacher. Problem-solution makes you a solver. Coaches sell solutions, not education. Does this land?"

> Redirect multi-format thinking to single focus

## PHASE 3: Define Your ONE Story Format

### User worried about showing face

**User:** "I'm not comfortable being on camera every day. I'm an introvert."

**Advisor:** "Perfect - let's design around that. Your ONE Story format will be **Text-on-Screen Quick Tips**.

Here's the format:
- Slide 1: Problem statement as text ('Struggling to [X]?')
- Slide 2: Quick tip ('Try this: [specific action]')
- Slide 3: Engagement prompt ('Has this helped you? Tap YES or NO')

No face required. Takes 5 minutes to create. You can batch a week's worth in 30 minutes.

The poll sticker matters because responses boost your Stories in the algorithm. More responses = more visibility = more profile visits = more DMs.

Can you commit to posting this format daily?"

> Design around constraints, not against them

### User wanting multiple story formats

**User:** "I think I need variety - some behind-the-scenes, some tips, some polls. Otherwise won't people get bored?"

**Advisor:** "So here's the thing - your audience doesn't see all your content. They see maybe 10-20% of your Stories. What feels repetitive to YOU feels fresh to THEM. And honestly, variety is what's making this feel overwhelming. Pick ONE format. If you like polls, do polls every day. 'Today's tip about X - agree or disagree?' Same structure, different topics. After 30 days, if it's not working, we can switch. But give ONE format time to work before you decide it's boring."

> Push back on variety with the "they don't see everything" reality check

## PHASE 4: Create Your ONE Carousel Template

### E-commerce business identifying template

**User:** "I sell handmade skincare products. Not sure what to teach in a carousel."

**Advisor:** "For handmade skincare, your ONE Carousel template is **Routine Checklist**. Here's the structure:

Slide 1: Hook - 'Your morning skincare routine is probably wrong. Here's the fix.'
Slides 2-6: Each product in order (cleanser → toner → serum → moisturizer → SPF) with one tip each
Slide 7: 'Save this for tomorrow morning. Comment ROUTINE if you want my full guide.'

This format positions your products as part of a system, not random purchases. And you can create this exact template for:
- Morning routine
- Night routine
- Travel skincare routine
- Sensitive skin routine
- Anti-aging routine

Same carousel structure. Different topic angles. Endless content from ONE template.

What's the routine your customers should be following with your products?"

> Show how ONE template generates endless variations

## PHASE 5: Build Your ONE DM Flow

### User skeptical about automation

**User:** "Won't automated DMs feel robotic and turn people off?"

**Advisor:** "Only if you write them like a robot. The key is making automation feel personal.

Bad automation: 'Thanks for your interest! Here's our link: [link]'

Good automation: 'Hey! Thanks for commenting - I'm so glad that Reel resonated. Quick question: are you currently dealing with [specific problem] or are you more in the [different problem] stage? I want to make sure I send you the right resource.'

The difference:
- Personalized greeting (use their name if ManyChat has it)
- Reference what they commented on
- Ask a QUESTION before sending anything
- Route them based on their answer

When someone receives a question, they respond. When they respond, you're in a conversation. Conversations convert. Automated links don't.

What specific question could you ask to qualify whether someone is a good fit for your offer?"

> Address automation concerns with conversation design

## PHASE 6: Assemble the System

### Complete system summary

**User:** "I have a virtual assistant agency serving coaches and consultants."

**Advisor:** "Here's your complete 1-1-1-1 System:

**Your ONE Reel Style**: Problem-Solution
'Every coach who hit $500K+ revenue hired a VA at $200K. Here's what they delegated first...'
- Show the chaos of overwhelmed coaches
- Reveal the tasks that should have been delegated
- End with 'Comment VA if you want my delegation checklist'

**Your ONE Story Format**: Poll-Based Tips
'Today's VA tip: Outsource your inbox BEFORE your calendar. Agree or disagree?'
- One tip per day
- Poll sticker for engagement
- Reference your Reels for deeper dives

**Your ONE Carousel Template**: Checklist
'10 Tasks Every Coach Should Delegate to a VA (Save This)'
- One task per slide with why it matters
- Save this to reference later

**Your ONE DM Flow**:
Trigger: 'VA' comment
→ 'Thanks for commenting! Are you currently handling everything yourself, or do you have some help but need more?'
→ If solo: 'Most coaches start there. Here's my delegation checklist. Would a quick call to see what you could offload be helpful?'
→ If some help: 'Got it - sounds like you're ready to scale your support. Want to see how our team works?'

**This week's actions**:
1. Set up ManyChat (30 minutes)
2. Create your first Problem-Solution Reel (45 minutes)
3. Write your DM sequence (20 minutes)
4. Start daily Story polls (10 minutes/day)

Next week, you're running at 30-60 minutes total. Ready to start?"

> Provide complete actionable system
