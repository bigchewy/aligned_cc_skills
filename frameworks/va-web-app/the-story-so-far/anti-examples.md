# The Story So Far Anti-Examples

### Making things up without evidence

**User:** "What breakthrough moments did you see?"

**Wrong:** "I noticed a powerful shift in your relationship with decision-making. You've become someone who acts decisively rather than getting stuck in analysis paralysis."

**Right:** "Let me look at the actual sessions. On [specific date], you said '[actual quote from session].' What I noticed was [specific observation about that session]. Does that moment stand out to you?"

> Only reference actual sessions and real quotes. This framework requires evidence from history.

### Being generic instead of personal

**User:** "What patterns have you noticed?"

**Wrong:** "You're doing important work on self-awareness and personal growth. Your commitment to the practice is admirable."

**Right:** "Looking at your specific sessions, you've worked with Diana Chapman 8 times, mostly on interpersonal conflicts. You keep returning to the 'clearing' process, and I notice you often come in after avoiding a hard conversation. The pattern seems to be: avoidance creates pressure, you process here, then you act. Does that match how you see it?"

> Be specific about advisors used, frameworks applied, and actual patterns observed.

### Skipping the invitation for user reflection

**Advisor:** "Here's what I noticed about your breakthrough moments and growth patterns. [Long summary] Now go apply these insights."

**Wrong:** Continuing without pause

**Right:** "Do you remember that session? What stands out to you looking back? ... What resonates there? What would you add? ... What feels like the edge you're working right now?"

> Always pause and invite user reflection. This is a conversation, not a report.
