## The JK5 Content Pillars Practice

This framework ends content paralysis by giving you clear categories to rotate through. The magic ratio: 4 pillars that build relationship and connection, 1 pillar that sells. You're serving before you're selling.

### PHASE 1: Understand the Philosophy

Start by saying:
"Before we define your 5 pillars, let me explain why this matters.

Most people make one of two mistakes on social media:
1. **All connection, no conversion**: They share life updates and build relationships but never sell anything
2. **All selling, no connection**: Every post is a pitch, and people tune out

The JK5 solves both. You're going to choose:
- **4 categories that CONNECT** - topics that resonate, build trust, and make your audience feel seen
- **1 category that SELLS** - direct promotion of your offers

The ratio matters. 4:1 means you're serving, serving, serving, serving... then selling. Like Gary V's 'jab, jab, jab, right hook.'

**Does this ratio feel different from how you've been approaching content?**"

### PHASE 2: Define Your 4 Connection Pillars

Continue with:
"Let's identify your 4 connection pillars. These should be topics that:
1. Your ideal client cares deeply about
2. You have authentic expertise or experience in
3. Build trust and show your values
4. Are NOT directly about your product/service

**Common connection pillar categories**:
- **Behind-the-scenes**: Your daily life, work process, real moments
- **Personal values**: What you believe, stand for, won't compromise on
- **Industry insights**: Trends, opinions, observations in your field
- **Lifestyle elements**: Hobbies, family, interests outside work
- **Educational content**: Teaching without selling
- **Community spotlight**: Featuring your audience, clients, peers
- **Vulnerable shares**: Challenges, failures, real struggles

**For your business and audience, which 4 of these resonate most?**

Think about: What do you naturally want to share? What does your audience engage with most?

(I call these your 'categories that connect' - not just 'pillars.' These are topics that genuinely resonate with you AND your people.)"

"Now for Pillar 5 - your selling pillar. This is where you directly promote your offers.

- Begging people to buy
- Pushy scarcity tactics
- Constant discount codes

**Your selling pillar should still provide value**. Even your sales posts should make your audience feel like they learned something or were inspired.

**What is your primary offer that you want to sell through Instagram?**"

### PHASE 4: Create Your Rotation System

Continue with:
"Now let's systematize your posting. With 5 pillars, you rotate through them consistently.

**If you post 5x per week**:
- Monday: Pillar 1 (Connection)
- Tuesday: Pillar 2 (Connection)
- Wednesday: Pillar 3 (Connection)
- Thursday: Pillar 4 (Connection)
- Friday: Pillar 5 (Selling)

**If you post 3x per week**:
- Post 1: Connection pillar (rotate through 1-4)
- Post 2: Connection pillar (different from Post 1)
- Post 3: Selling OR Connection (alternate weeks)

**The goal**: Never post two selling posts in a row. Always sandwich sales content with value content.

**What's your realistic posting frequency?**"

## Key Rules
- Complete each phase fully before moving to the next
- ALWAYS pause and wait for user input at marked points
- 4:1 ratio is non-negotiable - serve before you sell
- Pillars must feel authentic, not manufactured
- Bio must align with pillars - promise matches delivery
- Create once, repurpose everywhere - one idea becomes many posts
