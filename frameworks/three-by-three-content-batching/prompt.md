## The 3x3 Content Batching Practice

This framework eliminates daily content chaos. Instead of creating from scratch every day, you batch 90 days of content in one focused session. The result: 20 minutes per day on social media instead of hours.

### PHASE 1: Diagnose Your Content Chaos

Start by saying:
"Before we batch, I need to understand your current situation.

**Answer these honestly**:
1. How many hours per week do you spend on social media?
2. How many posts do you publish weekly across all platforms?
3. Can you take a week off and still have content posting?
4. Do you know what you're posting tomorrow?

If you're spending more than 2-3 hours per week AND can't answer 'yes' to questions 3 and 4, you have content chaos. You're creating reactively, not strategically.

**What are your answers to these four questions?**"

### PHASE 2: Define Your Content Themes

Continue with:
"We're going to define 4 content themes that you'll rotate through. This makes batching possible because you're not inventing topics - you're filling slots.

**Your 4 themes should cover**:
1. **Educational**: Teaching your audience something valuable
2. **Inspirational/Motivational**: Making them feel something
3. **Personal/Behind-the-Scenes**: Building connection
4. **Promotional**: Selling your offer (only 1 of 4!)

**For your business**, what would each theme look like?

**Example for a coach**:
- Educational: Tactical tips and frameworks
- Inspirational: Transformation stories, mindset shifts
- Personal: Daily life, journey, challenges
- Promotional: Client wins, open spots, offers

### PHASE 5: Batch Your Creation Session

Continue with:
"Now for the actual 3-hour batching session. Here's how to structure it:

**Hour 1: Educational + Inspirational Content**
- Write captions for 12 educational posts (5 min each)
- Write captions for 12 inspirational posts (5 min each)
- No overthinking - use your content bank ideas

**Hour 2: Personal + Promotional Content**
- Write captions for 12 personal posts (5 min each)
- Write captions for 12 promotional posts (5 min each)
- For personal: be authentic, not polished

**Hour 3: Visual Creation + Scheduling**
- Create or select images/videos for each post
- Schedule all 48 pieces into your scheduling tool
- Set up recurring time slots

**Tools for scheduling**:
- Later, Planoly, or Hootsuite for Instagram
- Buffer or native scheduling for other platforms

**When could you block 3 hours for your batching session?**"

"Here's how to maintain your content machine after batching:

**Daily (20 minutes)**:
- Respond to comments and DMs (15 min)
- Check scheduled posts look correct (2 min)

**Weekly (30 minutes)**:
- Review what performed well
- Note ideas for next batch
- Adjust any scheduled content if needed

**Quarterly (3 hours)**:
- Your next batching session
- Review analytics for what worked
- Update content bank with new ideas
