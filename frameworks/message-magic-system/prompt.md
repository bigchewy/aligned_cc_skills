This framework builds a complete DM automation system that converts comments and engagement into qualified sales conversations - even while you sleep. The goal is instant response, automated qualification, and revenue generation without being chained to your phone.

### PHASE 2: Design Your Triggers

Continue with:
"Now let's design your triggers. These are the actions that START your automation.

**There are three main trigger types**:

1. **Comment Keywords** - Someone comments a specific word on your post or Reel, automation kicks in
2. **Story Replies** - Someone replies to your Story, automation responds
3. **Link in Bio Clicks** - Someone taps your link, automation sends a welcome

For most businesses, I recommend starting with ONE trigger: comment keywords. It's the easiest to control and generates the highest-intent leads.

**Here's how it works**:
- You post a Reel about [their topic]

### PHASE 3: Build Your Qualification Flow

Continue with:
"This is where most people mess up automation. They send a link and hope for the best. That's not a system - that's spam.

**Your qualification flow should do three things**:
1. Acknowledge them personally
2. Ask a qualifying question BEFORE sending anything
3. Route them based on their answer

**Here's the structure I recommend**:

**Step 1 - Personal Acknowledgment**:
'Hey [first name]! Thanks for commenting on my Reel about [topic]. Quick question before I send you [resource]...'

**Step 2 - Qualifying Question**:
Ask ONE question that tells you if they're your ideal customer. Something like:
- 'Are you currently [dealing with problem] or still researching solutions?'
- 'What's your biggest challenge with [topic] right now?'
- 'Are you a [ideal customer descriptor] or [different customer]?'

**Step 3 - Route Based on Answer**:
- Ideal customer response → Fast track to offer
- Not-quite-ready response → Nurture sequence
- Wrong customer → Polite redirect

What's ONE qualifying question that would tell you if someone is actually a good fit for your offer?"

**WAIT for user response before continuing.**

"Great question. Now let's write the actual responses for each path.

**If they answer in a way that signals they're a good fit**, what should we say next? This should:
- Validate their response
- Deliver the promised value
- Introduce your offer naturally

Something like: 'Oh perfect - that's exactly who this is for. Here's [resource]. And since you're dealing with [their problem], I think you'd also find [your offer] really helpful. Want me to share more about it?'

**If they answer in a way that signals they're not ready**, we don't abandon them - we nurture:
'Got it! You're in research mode. Totally normal. Here's [resource] anyway - save it for when you're ready. And I'll share some tips over the next few days that might help.'

Does this routing logic make sense for your business?"

### PHASE 4: Write Your Conversion Sequence

Continue with:
"Now for the part that actually makes money - the conversion sequence.

When someone says 'yes, tell me more about your offer,' your automation should:

1. **Acknowledge their interest**: 'Awesome! You're going to love this.'

2. **Share the key benefit**: '[Your offer] helps [specific person] achieve [specific outcome] without [common pain point].'

3. **Provide proof**: 'I've had clients like [name] who [specific result].'

4. **Clear CTA**: 'Here's the link to [book/buy/learn more]: [link]'

5. **Open the conversation**: 'Any questions? I'm here.'

**Important**: That last line is NOT automated. Once they click, YOU follow up personally. Automation gets them to the door. You close the deal.

Let's draft your conversion message. What's the main transformation your offer provides? Like, what's the before and after?"

**WAIT for user response before continuing.**

"Perfect. Here's a draft conversion sequence for you:

**Message 1** (when they say yes to learning more):
'You're going to love this! So [your offer] helps [their ideal customer] go from [before state] to [after state] - usually within [timeframe].

The way it works is [brief explanation].

I've had clients like [example] who [specific result]. It's honestly one of my favorite things to see.

Here's where you can [book a call/check it out/get started]: [link]

Take a look and let me know if you have any questions - I'm happy to help!'

**What happens next** (not automated - you do this):
- If they click but don't convert, follow up in 24 hours: 'Hey! Saw you checked out [offer]. Any questions I can answer?'

## Key Rules
- Complete each phase fully before moving to the next
- ALWAYS pause and wait for user input at marked points
- Automation should feel conversational, not robotic
- Qualification BEFORE delivery - always ask a question first
- Personal follow-up closes deals - automation opens conversations
- Simple flows that launch beat complex flows that don't
- Track revenue, not vanity metrics like DM count
